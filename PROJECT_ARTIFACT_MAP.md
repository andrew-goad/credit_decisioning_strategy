## Two programs and two kinds of comparison

[Module 1 v2.0 SQL](Module_1_Synthetic_Application_%26_Risk_Modeling_Engine/v2.0/src/module1_synthetic_application_risk_engine_v2.0.sql) generates synthetic applications. Its parameter guide covers 50 fields. [Module 2 v1.0 SQL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/andrew-goad/credit_decisioning_strategy/65e7cc84528008ea40c6f9bb70a02745df592f4c/Module_2_Credit_Policy_Strategy_%26_Decision_Outcome_Simulation_Engine/v1.0/src/module2_credit_policy_strategy_decision_outcome_simulation_engine_v1.0.sql) applies the credit policy to a selected source slice and preserves the decision and its economics. The Module 2 guide identifies 91 fields across selectors, strategy and product settings, identity, archive controls and derived switches. Each guide distinguishes inputs you can change from values set by the program. [Search the parameter reference](https://andrew-goad.github.io/credit_decisioning_strategy/#parameter-reference)

The Power BI workbook compares two **versions of the generator** on 50,000 baseline applications. The teaching explorer compares **credit policies** within a source scenario. Both use synthetic data. The original generation logic, native report and historical campaign evidence remain available for independent review. [Module 1 v1.0 history](Module_1_Synthetic_Application_%26_Risk_Modeling_Engine/v1.0) · [Module 2 original SQL and raw download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/andrew-goad/credit_decisioning_strategy/65e7cc84528008ea40c6f9bb70a02745df592f4c/Module_2_Credit_Policy_Strategy_%26_Decision_Outcome_Simulation_Engine/v1.0/src/module2_credit_policy_strategy_decision_outcome_simulation_engine_v1.0.sql)

For historical v1.0 mortgage comparisons, the saved product profile covers 15,065 applications with a mean request of $344,327 and mean payment-to-income of 29.82%. The September review traces the earlier approximate figures and the evidence supporting these values.
