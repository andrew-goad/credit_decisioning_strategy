Module 1 · From synthetic application to expected loss

  1. Generate the applicant and the request

    The population ID fixes the random draws for each application ID. The scenario settings turn those draws into a product, score band, income, credit history, requested amount and terms.

  2. Price the request and calculate the monthly payment

    The product, borrower risk and rate environment shape the annual rate. The product determines which payment formula runs.

    • Mortgage, auto or personal loanSpread principal and interest over the loan term.
    • Revolving credit lineApply the minimum payment percentage plus a share of monthly interest.
    • Home equity lineEstimate an interest-only payment on the requested amount.

  3. Translate payment into default risk

    Divide monthly payment by gross monthly income. The resulting payment-to-income band modifies the score-based default estimate alongside borrower and product factors.

  4. Calculate loss and save the application

    Requested amount × modeled default probability × product loss given default. The saved scenario row gives Module 2 the original request, its affordability and its modeled loss before any policy decision.

Module 2 · From a credit request to a policy decision

  1. Match the inputs to a policy

    The run selector identifies a Module 1 scenario, a lending strategy and product rules. Preflight checks verify that the inputs and settings agree before the decision rules run.

  2. Evaluate the rules in priority order

    Policy stops and severe risk or debt-to-income triggers take precedence. The next tests identify counteroffer candidates, cases requiring review and requests eligible for approval. A case that clears none of these tests goes to review.

  3. Resolve the decision

    • ApproveOffer the requested amount and carry forward its modeled loss.
    • Test a counterofferSize and round a smaller offer. Check its minimum size, maximum reduction, product cap, payment burden and loss limit. A pass yields a counteroffer. A failure goes to review or decline according to policy.
    • Manual reviewLeave the amount and loss unresolved pending a decision.
    • DeclineRecord zero offered dollars and zero modeled loss on the offer.

  4. Explain, archive and compare

    Save each decision and its reasons. Match baseline and challenger decisions on the same applications, then compare offers, dollars and modeled losses. The completed campaign also checks run coverage, saved settings and outcome consistency.
