Alignment Scoring Framework

Use structured judgment rather than false precision.

Calculate four dimension scores from 0–100:

1. Functional Alignment — 50% of final score

Assess:

  • match to the role’s central mandate;
  • direct domain and product experience;
  • analytical and technical requirements;
  • scale and complexity;
  • leadership and stakeholder expectations;
  • regulated-environment requirements.

2. Screening Viability — 20%

Assess:

  • whether the résumé can prove the required years and keywords;
  • hard minimum qualifications;
  • title and level credibility;
  • likelihood that direct-versus-transferable distinctions survive recruiter and hiring-manager review;
  • severity of must-have gaps.

3. Practical Fit — 15%

Assess:

  • location and commute;
  • remote or hybrid model;
  • travel;
  • compensation;
  • schedule/time zone;
  • sponsorship or eligibility;
  • relocation expectations.

4. Strategic Career Value — 15%

Assess:

  • movement toward my intended market identity;
  • skill and domain growth;
  • quality of scope and leadership exposure;
  • brand and platform value;
  • compensation trajectory;
  • whether the role broadens or narrows future options.

Final Priority Score:

0.50 × Functional Alignment
+ 0.20 × Screening Viability
+ 0.15 × Practical Fit
+ 0.15 × Strategic Career Value

Always state a confidence level: High, Moderate, or Low.

  • Tier 1 — 84–89: Strong priority. Apply with disciplined tailoring.

  • Tier 2 — 70–77: Selective. Apply only with a referral, unusual strategic value, or low application cost.
  • Tier 3 — 60–69: Low expected return. Generally skip.
  • Below 60: No-go.

A hard disqualifier can override the numeric score.

Potential hard disqualifiers include:

  • required location or relocation I will not accept;
