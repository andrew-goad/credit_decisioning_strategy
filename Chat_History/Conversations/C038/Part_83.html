/***************************************************************************************************
SECTION 13 — ARCHIVE INVENTORY AND MATCHED STRATEGY COMPARISON FRAMEWORK
****************************************************************************************************

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
    Provide read-only archive inventory and governed matched strategy comparison diagnostics.

    Section 13 answers:

        "How do two archived Module 2 strategy runs differ when applied to the same Module 1
         scenario and population?"

    Section 13 does not answer:

        "Is the current latest-run output valid?"
        "Which strategy is best?"
        "Should either strategy be used in production?"
        "What should happen to a real applicant?"

    Section 13 is the archive comparison layer.

    Section 12 persists completed latest-run outputs into the archive.
    Section 13 inspects archived outputs and compares one selected baseline / challenger pair.

SQL PURPOSE:
    1. Confirm archive and comparison-registry dependencies exist.
    2. Load one active governed comparison from:
            credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_comparison_campaign_registry
    3. Materialize:
            tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_params
    4. Provide archive inventory diagnostics.
    5. Evaluate selected comparison readiness.
    6. Materialize matched baseline / challenger applicant rows into:
            tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_matched_rows
    7. Compare baseline and challenger outcomes across:
            portfolio-level metrics,
            decision migration,
            applicant-level deltas,
            product segments,
            score-band segments,
            product × score segments,
            reason-code movement,
            simulated offer-exposure transitions,
            approved exposure transitions,
            approved Expected Loss transitions.
    8. Validate immutable Module 1 source-field consistency in:
            tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_source_consistency
    9. Provide compact comparison-readiness and source-consistency diagnostics for Section 14.
    10. Avoid mutating archive history or recalculating strategy outcomes.

WHO SHOULD REVIEW THIS SECTION:
    Credit strategy reviewers:
        Confirm that the selected baseline / challenger comparison answers the intended strategy
        question and that observed deltas are interpreted as downstream Module 2 strategy movement.

    Model governance / validation reviewers:
        Confirm that comparison readiness, matched population discipline, and immutable source-field
        consistency are established before comparison deltas are used as validation evidence.

    SQL reviewers:
        Confirm that Section 13 reads from the archive, uses application_id for matched comparison,
        does not infer comparison selection from the latest-run table, and does not mutate permanent
        outputs.

    Portfolio / executive reviewers:
        Use the outputs to understand how strategy changes move simulated offer exposure, approved
        exposure, approved Expected Loss, decision mix, and reason-code themes.

WHEN TO RUN THIS SECTION:
    Run Section 13 after:

        Section 12 — Strategy Decision Archive Persistence Layer

    Section 13 should be run before:

        Section 14 — Module 2 Validation, QA, and Strategy Outcome Diagnostics

    Section 13 outputs are also useful for:

        Section 15 — Strategy Frontier Summary and Executive Comparison View
        Section 16 — Strategy Run Evidence Capture and Executive Story Register

INPUTS:
    credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions_archive
        Governed archive of completed Module 2 strategy runs.

    credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_comparison_campaign_registry
        Persistent governed comparison registry seeded in Section 2 and activated through the
        selected comparison group.

TEMPORARY OUTPUTS:
    tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_params
        One-row active comparison selector.

    tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_readiness
        One-row comparison readiness diagnostic.

    tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_matched_rows
        Applicant-level baseline / challenger matched comparison table.

    tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_source_consistency
        One-row immutable source-field consistency diagnostic.

GRAIN:
    Archive row-level grain:

        strategy_set_name
        strategy_name
        strategy_version
        module1_scenario_set_name
        module1_scenario_name
        population_id
        application_id

    Matched comparison grain:

        application_id

    within the same:

        module1_scenario_set_name
        module1_scenario_name
        population_id

WHY SECTION 13 USES THE ARCHIVE:
    credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions is the replaceable latest-run table.

    It changes whenever a new strategy run is materialized.

    A governed comparison requires two completed archived slices, so Section 13 reads from:

        credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions_archive

WHY SECTION 13 USES THE COMPARISON REGISTRY:
    A comparison should be selected deliberately.

    Inferring the baseline or challenger from the current latest-run table is unsafe because the
    latest-run table changes every time a run is executed.

    Section 13 therefore uses:

        credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_comparison_campaign_registry

    and materializes exactly one active comparison into:

        tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_params

WHY BASELINE AND CHALLENGER MUST SHARE MODULE 1 LINEAGE:
    Module 2 strategy comparison should isolate downstream strategy effects.

    In Module 2 V1.0, baseline and challenger must share:

        module1_scenario_set_name
        module1_scenario_name
        population_id

    Comparing different Module 1 scenarios would mix upstream population / rate / credit-box /
    exposure-intensity / LGD-severity effects with downstream Module 2 strategy effects.

WHY APPLICATION_ID IS THE MATCH KEY:
    application_id identifies the same synthetic application within the same Module 1 scenario and
    population.

    Matching by application_id lets reviewers observe how the same synthetic applicant moved from
    baseline outcome to challenger outcome.

WHY TIMESTAMPS AND STRATEGY_RUN_ID ARE NOT MATCH KEYS:
    strategy_run_id, strategy_run_ts, latest_output_created_ts, and archive_inserted_ts are audit
    metadata.

    They support traceability, but they do not define matched comparison grain.

WHY DECISION MIGRATION MATTERS:
    Decision migration shows how the challenger moved each synthetic applicant relative to baseline.

    Examples:
        APPROVE → COUNTEROFFER:
            access retained, but exposure reduced.

        APPROVE → DECLINE:
            risk or policy tightening.

        DECLINE → APPROVE:
            access expansion.

        MANUAL_REVIEW → DECLINE:
            review-burden reduction through final no-offer routing.

WHY REQUESTED AND APPROVED EXPECTED LOSS ARE BOTH COMPARED:
    requested_expected_loss_amount is requested-exposure Expected Loss from Module 1.

    approved_expected_loss_amount is approved-exposure Expected Loss after Module 2 final exposure
    treatment.

    Requested Expected Loss helps confirm source-risk consistency.
    Approved Expected Loss shows downstream strategy impact after approvals, counteroffers, manual
    reviews, and declines are resolved.

WHY SOURCE-FIELD CONSISTENCY IS VALIDATED:
    Matched comparison should isolate Module 2 strategy effects.

    If immutable Module 1 fields differ between baseline and challenger rows, the comparison may
    reflect source-selection error, population drift, upstream scenario mismatch, or archive
    corruption rather than true Module 2 strategy movement.

    Section 13 therefore compares baseline and challenger values for:
        product_type_policy_key
        score_band_policy_key
        credit_score
        annual_income
        requested_amount
        requested_monthly_payment_proxy
        requested_payment_to_income_ratio
        amount_to_income_ratio
        estimated_pd
        estimated_lgd
        requested_expected_loss_amount
        module1_scenario_set_name
        module1_scenario_name
        population_id

    using IS DISTINCT FROM for NULL-safe comparison.

READINESS STATUS INTERPRETATION:
    READY_FOR_MATCHED_COMPARISON:
        Baseline and challenger slices exist, are unique at application_id, share the selected Module 1
        scenario / population, and fully overlap by application_id.

    Any other readiness status:
        Inventory outputs are still useful, but matched comparison deltas should not be interpreted as
        valid baseline / challenger movement.

SOURCE CONSISTENCY STATUS INTERPRETATION:
    SOURCE_FIELDS_MATCH:
        Immutable Module 1 fields match across matched baseline / challenger rows.

    SOURCE_FIELD_MISMATCH_REVIEW_REQUIRED:
        Do not interpret strategy deltas as pure Module 2 strategy movement until source mismatches
        are reviewed.

    NOT_APPLICABLE_NOT_READY_FOR_MATCHED_COMPARISON:
        Selected comparison was not ready, so source-field consistency cannot be evaluated.

SIMULATED OFFER EXPOSURE BOUNDARY:
    simulated_offer_exposure_flag identifies simulated positive offer exposure.

    It is TRUE for:
        APPROVE
        COUNTEROFFER

    It is FALSE for:
        MANUAL_REVIEW
        DECLINE

    It does not imply:
        booked exposure,
        funded exposure,
        account creation,
        production approval,
        customer treatment,
        or real applicant treatment.

REASON-CODE BOUNDARY:
    Reason-code movement explains internal simulated strategy explanation themes.

    It is not:
        customer communication,
        regulatory notice language,
        adverse-action language,
        fair-lending conclusion,
        or production compliance evidence.

SECTION 13 IS NOT THE FULL QA FRAMEWORK:
    Section 13 compares archived strategy slices.

    Section 14 validates the completed latest-run output.

    Section 16 persists executive and validation evidence.

SQL CONCEPTS TAUGHT IN THIS SECTION:
    Registry-selected comparison:
        Uses persistent comparison metadata rather than latest-run inference.

    Archive grain:
        Compares completed historical strategy slices.

    FULL OUTER JOIN:
        Measures overlap and baseline-only / challenger-only application_id values.

    Application-level matched join:
        Pairs the same synthetic applicant across two strategies.

    COUNT(*) FILTER:
        Creates concise conditional counts for outcome and diagnostic metrics.

    NULLIF:
        Prevents divide-by-zero in rate calculations.

    COALESCE:
        Treats unresolved / no-offer approved exposure and approved Expected Loss as zero for
        all-application portfolio-impact metrics.

    IS DISTINCT FROM:
        Compares nullable fields safely.

    CASE:
        Classifies readiness status, migration type, and source-consistency status.

READ-ONLY BOUNDARY:
    Section 13 does not insert archive rows.
    Section 13 does not delete archive rows.
    Section 13 does not update archive rows.
    Section 13 does not modify latest-run rows.
    Section 13 does not recalculate decision_outcome.
    Section 13 does not recalculate approved exposure.
    Section 13 does not recalculate approved Expected Loss.
    Section 13 does not reassign simulated reason codes.
    Section 13 does not modify strategy parameters.
    Section 13 does not modify product-policy parameters.
    Section 13 does not insert evidence rows.
    Section 13 does not draft documentation.

***************************************************************************************************/

/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.0A Persistent archive and comparison-registry dependency gatekeeper.

Business purpose:
    Fail clearly if the archive or comparison registry required for governed comparison is missing.

SQL mechanics:
    TO_REGCLASS checks whether persistent tables exist.
    information_schema.columns checks whether the comparison registry has the expected schema.
    COUNT(*) validates active comparison cardinality.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

DO $$
DECLARE
    v_archive_exists BOOLEAN;
    v_registry_exists BOOLEAN;
    v_required_registry_column_gap_count BIGINT;
    v_active_comparison_count BIGINT;
    v_placeholder_count BIGINT;
    v_same_strategy_count BIGINT;
BEGIN
    /***********************************************************************************************
    Archive dependency check.

    Section 13 can summarize an empty archive, but the archive table itself must exist.
    Section 12 creates / reuses credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions_archive.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT TO_REGCLASS('credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions_archive') IS NOT NULL
    INTO v_archive_exists;

    IF v_archive_exists IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 13 failure: strategy_decisions_archive does not exist. Run Section 12 archive setup / persistence before Section 13.';
    END IF;


    /***********************************************************************************************
    Comparison registry dependency check.

    Comparison selection should come from the persistent comparison campaign registry, not from
    latest-run inference.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT TO_REGCLASS('credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_comparison_campaign_registry') IS NOT NULL
    INTO v_registry_exists;

    IF v_registry_exists IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 13 failure: strategy_comparison_campaign_registry does not exist. Run Section 2 registry setup before Section 13.';
    END IF;


    /***********************************************************************************************
    Registry schema check.

    Business purpose:
        Section 13 needs comparison IDs, run IDs, strategy keys, Module 1 lineage, story metadata,
        and active flags.

    SQL mechanics:
        The VALUES list defines required column names.
        NOT EXISTS flags missing registry fields.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_required_registry_column_gap_count
    FROM (
        VALUES
            ('comparison_group_id'),
            ('comparison_set_name'),
            ('showcase_id'),
            ('showcase_name'),
            ('baseline_module2_run_id'),
            ('challenger_module2_run_id'),
            ('baseline_strategy_set_name'),
            ('baseline_strategy_name'),
            ('baseline_strategy_version'),
            ('challenger_strategy_set_name'),
            ('challenger_strategy_name'),
            ('challenger_strategy_version'),
            ('module1_scenario_set_name'),
            ('module1_scenario_name'),
            ('population_id'),
            ('expected_comparison_storyline'),
            ('primary_metric_focus'),
            ('active_comparison_flag'),
            ('registry_active_flag'),
            ('registry_created_ts'),
            ('registry_updated_ts')
    ) AS required_columns(column_name)
    WHERE NOT EXISTS (
        SELECT 1
        FROM information_schema.columns c
        WHERE c.table_schema = 'credit_decisioning_sim'
          AND c.table_name = 'strategy_comparison_campaign_registry'
          AND c.column_name = required_columns.column_name
    );

    IF v_required_registry_column_gap_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 13 failure: strategy_comparison_campaign_registry is missing required column(s).';
    END IF;


    /***********************************************************************************************
    Active comparison cardinality.

    Expected:
        exactly one row where:
            active_comparison_flag = TRUE
            registry_active_flag = TRUE

    Business purpose:
        Section 13 should compare one selected baseline / challenger pair at a time.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_active_comparison_count
    FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_comparison_campaign_registry
    WHERE active_comparison_flag IS TRUE
      AND registry_active_flag IS TRUE;

    IF v_active_comparison_count <> 1 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 13 failure: expected exactly one active comparison registry row, found %.',
            v_active_comparison_count;
    END IF;


    /***********************************************************************************************
    Placeholder protection.

    Business purpose:
        Prevent accidental interpretation of placeholder registry metadata as real comparison
        evidence.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_placeholder_count
    FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_comparison_campaign_registry
    WHERE active_comparison_flag IS TRUE
      AND registry_active_flag IS TRUE
      AND (
             comparison_group_id ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR comparison_set_name ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR baseline_module2_run_id ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR challenger_module2_run_id ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR baseline_strategy_set_name ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR baseline_strategy_name ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR baseline_strategy_version ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR challenger_strategy_set_name ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR challenger_strategy_name ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
          OR challenger_strategy_version ILIKE '%PLACEHOLDER%'
      );

    IF v_placeholder_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 13 failure: active comparison registry row contains placeholder comparison or strategy identifiers.';
    END IF;


    /***********************************************************************************************
    Same-strategy protection.

    Business purpose:
        A baseline / challenger comparison should compare different Module 2 strategy identities.

        If this fires, the selected comparison does not test strategy movement.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_same_strategy_count
    FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_comparison_campaign_registry
    WHERE active_comparison_flag IS TRUE
      AND registry_active_flag IS TRUE
      AND baseline_strategy_set_name = challenger_strategy_set_name
      AND baseline_strategy_name = challenger_strategy_name
      AND baseline_strategy_version = challenger_strategy_version;

    IF v_same_strategy_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 13 failure: active comparison selects the same strategy as both baseline and challenger.';
    END IF;
END $$;

/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.1A Archive inventory by strategy / Module 1 scenario / population.

Expected:
    One row per archived strategy / scenario / population slice.

Interpretation:
    Use this output to confirm the selected baseline and challenger slices are present.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    strategy_set_name,
    strategy_name,
    strategy_version,

    module1_scenario_set_name,
    module1_scenario_name,
    population_id,

    COUNT(*) AS archived_rows,
    COUNT(DISTINCT application_id) AS distinct_application_id_count,

    COUNT(*) - COUNT(DISTINCT application_id)
        AS duplicate_application_row_count,

    MIN(strategy_run_id) AS min_strategy_run_id,
    MAX(strategy_run_id) AS max_strategy_run_id,

    MIN(strategy_run_ts) AS min_strategy_run_ts,
    MAX(strategy_run_ts) AS max_strategy_run_ts,

    MIN(archive_inserted_ts) AS first_archive_inserted_ts,
    MAX(archive_inserted_ts) AS last_archive_inserted_ts

FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions_archive

GROUP BY
    strategy_set_name,
    strategy_name,
    strategy_version,
    module1_scenario_set_name,
    module1_scenario_name,
    population_id

ORDER BY
    module1_scenario_set_name,
    module1_scenario_name,
    population_id,
    strategy_set_name,
    strategy_name,
    strategy_version;



/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.1B Archived decision-outcome inventory by slice.

Purpose:
    Show the final outcome mix for every archived slice.

Interpretation:
    This is inventory, not matched comparison.

    The same decision_outcome distribution can mean different things depending on product, score,
    requested exposure, affordability, and reason-code mix.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    strategy_set_name,
    strategy_name,
    strategy_version,

    module1_scenario_set_name,
    module1_scenario_name,
    population_id,

    decision_outcome,

    COUNT(*) AS archived_rows,

    ROUND(
        100.0 * COUNT(*)::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(
            SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (
                PARTITION BY
                    strategy_set_name,
                    strategy_name,
                    strategy_version,
                    module1_scenario_set_name,
                    module1_scenario_name,
                    population_id
            ),
            0
        ),
        2
    ) AS pct_within_archived_slice

FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions_archive

GROUP BY
    strategy_set_name,
    strategy_name,
    strategy_version,
    module1_scenario_set_name,
    module1_scenario_name,
    population_id,
    decision_outcome

ORDER BY
    module1_scenario_set_name,
    module1_scenario_name,
    population_id,
    strategy_set_name,
    strategy_name,
    strategy_version,
    CASE decision_outcome
        WHEN 'APPROVE' THEN 1
        WHEN 'COUNTEROFFER' THEN 2
        WHEN 'MANUAL_REVIEW' THEN 3
        WHEN 'DECLINE' THEN 4
        ELSE 99
    END;



/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.1C Archive row-level grain duplicate diagnostic.

Purpose:
    Confirm the archive is unique at:
        strategy_set_name
        strategy_name
        strategy_version
        module1_scenario_set_name
        module1_scenario_name

Expected:

    Duplicate archive grain can corrupt matched comparison and frontier analysis.

SELECT

FROM (
    SELECT
        strategy_set_name,
        strategy_name,
        strategy_version,

        module1_scenario_name,

    GROUP BY
        strategy_set_name,
        strategy_name,
        strategy_version,
        module1_scenario_set_name,
        module1_scenario_name,
        population_id,
        application_id

    HAVING COUNT(*) > 1
) d;



/***************************************************************************************************
SECTION 13.2 — SELECTED COMPARISON READINESS
****************************************************************************************************

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
    Evaluate whether the selected baseline / challenger comparison is ready for matched comparison.

    Section 13.2 answers:

        "Do the selected baseline and challenger archived slices exist, have clean application grain,
         and fully overlap by application_id?"

SQL PURPOSE:
    1. Count archived baseline rows.
    2. Count archived challenger rows.
    3. Count distinct application_id values for each side.
    4. Count duplicate application_id rows within each side.
    5. Count matched, baseline-only, and challenger-only application_id values.
    6. Assign readiness_status.
    7. Persist the readiness result into tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_readiness.

IMPORTANT:
    Section 13.2 does not fail solely because selected archive slices are not ready.

    It records readiness status so inventory can still be reviewed and Section 14 can surface
    NOT_APPLICABLE comparison status where appropriate.

***************************************************************************************************/

CREATE TEMP TABLE tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_readiness
ON COMMIT PRESERVE ROWS
AS
WITH params AS (
    /***********************************************************************************************
    One-row active comparison selector from Section 13.0.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT *
    FROM tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_params
    LIMIT 1
),

baseline_slice AS (
    /***********************************************************************************************
    Baseline archived slice selected by baseline strategy identity and Module 1 lineage.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT
        b.application_id
    FROM params p

    LEFT JOIN credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions_archive b
        ON b.strategy_set_name = p.baseline_strategy_set_name
       AND b.strategy_name = p.baseline_strategy_name
       AND b.strategy_version = p.baseline_strategy_version
       AND b.module1_scenario_set_name = p.module1_scenario_set_name
       AND b.module1_scenario_name = p.module1_scenario_name
       AND b.population_id = p.population_id
),

challenger_slice AS (
    /***********************************************************************************************
    Challenger archived slice selected by challenger strategy identity and the same Module 1 lineage.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT
        c.application_id

    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT
        COUNT(application_id) AS baseline_archived_row_count,

        COUNT(application_id) - COUNT(DISTINCT application_id)
            AS baseline_duplicate_application_row_count
    FROM baseline_slice
),

    /***********************************************************************************************
    Challenger row and grain diagnostics.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT
        COUNT(application_id) AS challenger_archived_row_count,
        COUNT(DISTINCT application_id) AS challenger_distinct_application_id_count,
        COUNT(application_id) - COUNT(DISTINCT application_id)
            AS challenger_duplicate_application_row_count
    FROM challenger_slice

    /***********************************************************************************************
    Application-level overlap diagnostics.

    SQL mechanics:
        FULL OUTER JOIN keeps application IDs that appear on either side.

    Business interpretation:
        Complete overlap is required for clean matched comparison.
    ***********************************************************************************************/
    SELECT
        COUNT(*) FILTER (
            WHERE b.application_id IS NOT NULL
              AND c.application_id IS NOT NULL
        ) AS matched_application_id_count,

        COUNT(*) FILTER (
            WHERE b.application_id IS NOT NULL
              AND c.application_id IS NULL

            WHERE b.application_id IS NULL
              AND c.application_id IS NOT NULL
        ) AS challenger_only_application_id_count

    FROM (
        SELECT DISTINCT application_id
        FROM baseline_slice
        WHERE application_id IS NOT NULL
    ) b

    FULL OUTER JOIN (
        SELECT DISTINCT application_id
        FROM challenger_slice
        WHERE application_id IS NOT NULL
    ) c
        ON c.application_id = b.application_id

    p.showcase_name,

        AND p.baseline_strategy_version = p.challenger_strategy_version

    cc.challenger_duplicate_application_row_count,

    ao.matched_application_id_count,
    ao.baseline_only_application_id_count,
    ao.challenger_only_application_id_count,

    CASE
        WHEN (
            p.baseline_strategy_set_name = p.challenger_strategy_set_name
            AND p.baseline_strategy_name = p.challenger_strategy_name
            AND p.baseline_strategy_version = p.challenger_strategy_version
        )
            THEN 'NOT_READY_SAME_STRATEGY_SELECTED'

        WHEN bc.baseline_archived_row_count = 0
         AND cc.challenger_archived_row_count = 0
            THEN 'NOT_READY_BASELINE_AND_CHALLENGER_ARCHIVE_MISSING'

        WHEN bc.baseline_archived_row_count = 0
            THEN 'NOT_READY_BASELINE_ARCHIVE_MISSING'

        WHEN cc.challenger_archived_row_count = 0
            THEN 'NOT_READY_CHALLENGER_ARCHIVE_MISSING'

        WHEN bc.baseline_duplicate_application_row_count > 0
          OR cc.challenger_duplicate_application_row_count > 0
            THEN 'NOT_READY_DUPLICATE_APPLICATION_ID_IN_ARCHIVE_SLICE'

        WHEN bc.baseline_archived_row_count <> bc.baseline_distinct_application_id_count
          OR cc.challenger_archived_row_count <> cc.challenger_distinct_application_id_count
            THEN 'NOT_READY_ARCHIVE_SLICE_NOT_UNIQUE_AT_APPLICATION_ID'

        WHEN bc.baseline_distinct_application_id_count <> cc.challenger_distinct_application_id_count
            THEN 'NOT_READY_BASELINE_CHALLENGER_ROW_COUNT_MISMATCH'

        WHEN ao.matched_application_id_count <> bc.baseline_distinct_application_id_count
          OR ao.matched_application_id_count <> cc.challenger_distinct_application_id_count
          OR ao.baseline_only_application_id_count > 0
          OR ao.challenger_only_application_id_count > 0
            THEN 'NOT_READY_INCOMPLETE_APPLICATION_ID_OVERLAP'

        ELSE 'READY_FOR_MATCHED_COMPARISON'
    END AS readiness_status,

    p.expected_comparison_storyline,
    p.primary_metric_focus

FROM params p
CROSS JOIN baseline_counts bc
CROSS JOIN challenger_counts cc
CROSS JOIN application_overlap ao;

/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.4A Baseline and challenger portfolio summary.

Interpretation:
    This is the high-level matched comparison table.

    Review rates and totals before drilling into migration, segments, reason codes, and source
    consistency.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

WITH side_summary AS (
    SELECT
        'BASELINE'::TEXT AS comparison_side,

        COUNT(*) AS applications,

        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE') AS approval_count,
        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER') AS counteroffer_count,
        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW') AS manual_review_count,
        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE') AS decline_count,
        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE) AS offer_count,

        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE')::NUMERIC
            / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS approval_rate,

        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')::NUMERIC
            / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS counteroffer_rate,

        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW')::NUMERIC
            / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS manual_review_rate,

        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE')::NUMERIC
            / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS decline_rate,

        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE baseline_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)::NUMERIC
            / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS simulated_offer_exposure_rate,

        SUM(baseline_requested_amount) AS total_requested_amount,
        SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_amount, 0)) AS total_approved_amount,

        SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_amount, 0))
            / NULLIF(SUM(baseline_requested_amount), 0) AS approved_amount_capture_rate,

        SUM(baseline_requested_expected_loss_amount)
            AS total_requested_expected_loss_amount,

        SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
            AS total_approved_expected_loss_amount,

        SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
            / NULLIF(SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_amount, 0)), 0)
            AS approved_expected_loss_rate,

        AVG(baseline_requested_payment_to_income_ratio)
            AS avg_requested_payment_to_income_ratio,

        AVG(baseline_approved_payment_to_income_ratio)

/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.9A Simulated offer-exposure flag transition.

Interpretation:
    FALSE → TRUE:
        access expansion.

    TRUE → FALSE:
        risk tightening or review / no-offer shift.

    TRUE → TRUE:
        retained simulated positive offer exposure, possibly with changed approved amount.

Boundary:
    simulated_offer_exposure_flag is simulated positive offer exposure, not booked exposure.

SELECT
    baseline_simulated_offer_exposure_flag,
    challenger_simulated_offer_exposure_flag,

    CASE
        WHEN baseline_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE
         AND challenger_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE
            THEN 'OFFER_EXPOSURE_RETAINED'

        WHEN baseline_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS FALSE
         AND challenger_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE
            THEN 'OFFER_EXPOSURE_GAINED'

        WHEN baseline_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE
         AND challenger_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS FALSE
            THEN 'OFFER_EXPOSURE_REMOVED'

        ELSE 'NO_OFFER_EXPOSURE_BOTH'
    END AS offer_exposure_transition_type,

    COUNT(*) AS row_count,

    ROUND(
        100.0 * COUNT(*)::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (), 0),
        2

    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_amount, 0)) AS total_challenger_approved_amount,

    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_amount, 0))
        - SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_amount, 0))
        AS delta_total_approved_amount,

    SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        AS total_baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount,

    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        AS total_challenger_approved_expected_loss_amount,

    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        - SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        AS delta_total_approved_expected_loss_amount

FROM tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_matched_rows

GROUP BY
    baseline_simulated_offer_exposure_flag,
    challenger_simulated_offer_exposure_flag

    offer_exposure_transition_type;



/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.9B Approved exposure movement by decision migration type.

Purpose:
    Connect outcome migration to exposure movement.

Interpretation:
    Large exposure reduction with retained offer exposure may indicate counteroffer management.
    Exposure removal may indicate risk discipline or manual-review / decline migration.

SELECT
    decision_migration_type,

    COUNT(*) AS row_count,

    SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_amount, 0)) AS total_baseline_approved_amount,
    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_amount, 0)) AS total_challenger_approved_amount,

    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_amount, 0))
        - SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_amount, 0))
        AS delta_total_approved_amount,

    AVG(delta_approved_amount) AS avg_delta_approved_amount,

    AVG(baseline_approved_payment_to_income_ratio)
        FILTER (WHERE baseline_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)
        AS avg_baseline_approved_pti_offer_rows,

    AVG(challenger_approved_payment_to_income_ratio)
        FILTER (WHERE challenger_simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)
        AS avg_challenger_approved_pti_offer_rows

FROM tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_matched_rows

GROUP BY decision_migration_type

ORDER BY
    ABS(SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_amount, 0))
        - SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_amount, 0))) DESC,
    decision_migration_type;



/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13.9C Approved Expected Loss movement by decision migration type.

Purpose:
    Connect outcome migration to approved-exposure Expected Loss movement.

Boundary:
    approved_expected_loss_amount is a synthetic strategy metric, not a production loss forecast.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    decision_migration_type,

    COUNT(*) AS row_count,

    SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        AS total_baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount,

    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        AS total_challenger_approved_expected_loss_amount,

    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        - SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        AS delta_total_approved_expected_loss_amount,

    AVG(delta_approved_expected_loss_amount)
        AS avg_delta_approved_expected_loss_amount,

    SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        / NULLIF(SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_amount, 0)), 0)
        AS baseline_approved_expected_loss_rate,

    SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        / NULLIF(SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_amount, 0)), 0)
        AS challenger_approved_expected_loss_rate

FROM tmp_m2_strategy_comparison_matched_rows

GROUP BY decision_migration_type

ORDER BY
    ABS(
        SUM(COALESCE(challenger_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
        - SUM(COALESCE(baseline_approved_expected_loss_amount, 0))
    ) DESC,
    decision_migration_type;
