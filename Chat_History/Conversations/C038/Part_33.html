SECTION 14.11 — COUNTEROFFER VALIDATION DIAGNOSTICS
****************************************************************************************************

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
    Validate that final COUNTEROFFER outcomes are feasible, explainable, and consistent with the
    governed exposure-treatment framework.

    Section 14.11 answers:

        "When Module 2 assigns a final COUNTEROFFER outcome, did the row pass reduced-exposure
         feasibility testing, and does the final exposure treatment behave as intended?"

SQL PURPOSE:
    1. Summarize preliminary and final counteroffer populations.
    2. Calculate counteroffer feasibility pass / fail rates.
    3. Review final counteroffer exposure reduction, requested PTI, approved PTI, requested Expected
       Loss, approved Expected Loss, and approved Expected Loss reduction.
    4. Review failed preliminary counteroffer reasons.
    5. Review counteroffer feasibility by product and score band.
    6. Validate final counteroffer conventions.
    7. Review final COUNTEROFFER simulated reason-code distribution.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION:
    Final COUNTEROFFER rows should show:
        approved_amount < requested_amount;
        exposure_reduction_pct > 0;
        counteroffer_amount = approved_amount;
        approved_payment_to_income_ratio <= counteroffer_target_pti;
        approved_expected_loss_amount <= product_approved_el_cap;
        counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag = TRUE;
        primary_reason_family = COUNTEROFFER.

WHY PRELIMINARY AND FINAL COUNTEROFFERS ARE BOTH REVIEWED:
    Preliminary COUNTEROFFER routing means:

        "This row should be tested for reduced-exposure feasibility."

    Final COUNTEROFFER means:
        Section 8 confirmed feasibility,
        Section 9 resolved the row to final COUNTEROFFER,
        and Section 10 assigned a counteroffer-family simulated reason code.

    Reviewing both populations reveals:
        how many rows were sent for feasibility testing,
        how many passed,
        how many failed,
        and how final counteroffer outcomes differ by product and score band.

COUNTEROFFER FEASIBILITY BOUNDARY:
    counteroffer_feasibility_failure_reason is a feasibility diagnostic.

    It is not a final simulated reason code.
    It is not external communication text.
    It is not a production decline reason.

COUNTEROFFER OUTCOME BOUNDARY:
    COUNTEROFFER is a simulated positive offer-exposure outcome for synthetic applicants.

    It does not imply:
        operational negotiation,
        booked exposure,
        funded exposure,
        production approval,
        operational account creation,
        customer communication,
        or real applicant treatment.

APPROVED PTI BOUNDARY:
    approved_payment_to_income_ratio is a Module 2 strategy-simulation affordability proxy.

    It is not a contractual payment ratio.
    It is not a servicing calculation.
    It is not a billing-engine output.

EXPECTED LOSS BOUNDARY:
    requested_expected_loss_amount is requested-exposure Expected Loss.

    approved_expected_loss_amount is approved-exposure Expected Loss after final simulated exposure
    treatment.

    Section 14.11 validates behavior and conventions. It does not recalculate Expected Loss.

REASON-CODE BOUNDARY:
    COUNTEROFFER reason codes are simulated strategy explanation codes.

    They are used for QA, transparency, and strategy interpretation.

    They are not regulatory notices, customer communications, adverse-action reasons, or fair-lending
    conclusions.

***************************************************************************************************/


/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.11A Counteroffer summary.

Purpose:
    Provide the primary counteroffer performance and feasibility profile for the latest-run output.

Interpretation:
    preliminary_counteroffer_rows:
        rows routed to reduced-exposure feasibility testing.

    feasible_counteroffers:
        preliminary COUNTEROFFER rows that passed feasibility testing.

    failed_counteroffers:
        preliminary COUNTEROFFER rows that failed feasibility testing.

    final_counteroffer_rows:
        rows that resolved to final COUNTEROFFER after feasibility and final decision resolution.

    counteroffer_feasibility_pass_rate:
        feasibility pass rate among preliminary COUNTEROFFER rows.

Documentation use:
    This is the headline counteroffer QA table. It supports explanation of how many rows were
    counteroffer candidates, how many became feasible counteroffers, and what affordability / Expected
    Loss improvement was achieved.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS final_counteroffer_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS preliminary_counteroffer_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
    ) AS feasible_counteroffers,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS FALSE
    ) AS failed_counteroffers,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
    )::NUMERIC
    / NULLIF(
        COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'),
        0
    ) AS counteroffer_feasibility_pass_rate,

    AVG(exposure_reduction_pct) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_exposure_reduction_pct_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(requested_payment_to_income_ratio) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_requested_pti_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(approved_payment_to_income_ratio) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_approved_pti_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(
        requested_payment_to_income_ratio - approved_payment_to_income_ratio
    ) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_pti_reduction_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(requested_expected_loss_amount) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_requested_expected_loss_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(approved_expected_loss_amount) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_approved_expected_loss_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(approved_el_reduction_pct) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_approved_el_reduction_pct_for_final_counteroffers

FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions;


/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.11B Counteroffer feasibility failure reason distribution.

Purpose:
    Show why preliminary COUNTEROFFER rows failed feasibility testing.

Interpretation:
    These are Section 8 feasibility diagnostics only.

    They help explain failed preliminary counteroffers before final decision resolution converts them
    to MANUAL_REVIEW or DECLINE according to failed_counteroffer_resolution.

Expected use:
    Review whether failures are concentrated in:
        PTI target constraints,
        approved Expected Loss caps,
        product minimums,
        maximum exposure reduction,
        product counteroffer permissions,
        or rounding / raw amount feasibility.

Boundary:
    counteroffer_feasibility_failure_reason is not a final reason code and not external
    communication language.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    counteroffer_feasibility_failure_reason,

    COUNT(*) AS row_count,

    ROUND(
        100.0 * COUNT(*)::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (), 0),
        2
    ) AS pct_of_failed_preliminary_counteroffers

FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions

WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
  AND counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS FALSE

GROUP BY counteroffer_feasibility_failure_reason

ORDER BY
    row_count DESC,
    counteroffer_feasibility_failure_reason;


/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.11C Product × counteroffer feasibility summary.

Purpose:
    Review how counteroffer feasibility varies by product.

Interpretation:
    Product-specific policy controls can affect feasibility through:
        product_max_approved_amount,
        product_min_approved_amount,
        product_min_counteroffer_pct,
        product_max_exposure_reduction_pct,
        counteroffer_target_pti,
        product_approved_el_cap,
        approved_amount_rounding_increment.

    Higher failed-counteroffer rates by product are not automatically defects.

    They should be explained through product guardrails, requested exposure, affordability pressure,
    approved Expected Loss caps, and reason-code patterns.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    product_type_policy_key,
    counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag,

    COUNT(*) AS preliminary_counteroffer_rows,

    AVG(requested_amount) AS avg_requested_amount,

    AVG(approved_amount) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_approved_amount_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(exposure_reduction_pct) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_exposure_reduction_pct_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(requested_payment_to_income_ratio) AS avg_requested_payment_to_income_ratio,

    AVG(approved_payment_to_income_ratio) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_approved_pti_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(requested_expected_loss_amount) AS avg_requested_expected_loss_amount,

    AVG(approved_expected_loss_amount) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_approved_expected_loss_for_final_counteroffers

FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions

WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'

GROUP BY
    product_type_policy_key,
    counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag

ORDER BY
    product_type_policy_key,
    counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag;


/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.11D Score band × counteroffer feasibility summary.

Purpose:
    Review how counteroffer feasibility varies by score-band segment.

Interpretation:
    Score-band differences in counteroffer feasibility may reflect:
        synthetic estimated-risk proxy level,
        requested Expected Loss,
        product mix,
        requested PTI,
        approved Expected Loss cap pressure,
        or requested exposure intensity.

    This query helps identify whether counteroffer feasibility is concentrated in mid-risk bands,
    lower-risk bands with exposure pressure, or weaker bands with affordability / Expected Loss
    pressure.

Boundary:
    estimated_pd remains the SQL field name and is interpreted as the synthetic estimated-PD proxy.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    score_band_policy_key,
    counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag,

    COUNT(*) AS preliminary_counteroffer_rows,

    AVG(estimated_pd) AS avg_synthetic_estimated_pd_proxy,
    AVG(requested_payment_to_income_ratio) AS avg_requested_payment_to_income_ratio,

    AVG(approved_payment_to_income_ratio) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_approved_pti_for_final_counteroffers,

    AVG(requested_expected_loss_amount) AS avg_requested_expected_loss_amount,

    AVG(approved_expected_loss_amount) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS avg_approved_expected_loss_for_final_counteroffers

FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions

WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'

GROUP BY
    score_band_policy_key,
    counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag

ORDER BY
    CASE score_band_policy_key
        WHEN 'SUPER_PRIME' THEN 1
        WHEN 'PRIME' THEN 2
        WHEN 'NEAR_PRIME' THEN 3
        WHEN 'SUBPRIME' THEN 4
        WHEN 'DEEP_SUBPRIME' THEN 5
        ELSE 99
    END,
    counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag;


/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.11E Final counteroffer convention diagnostic.

Purpose:
    Provide compact violation counts for final COUNTEROFFER conventions.

Expected result:
    all violation counts should be zero.

Validation conventions:
    Final COUNTEROFFER rows should:
        pass feasibility,
        have approved_amount populated,
        have approved_amount > 0,
        have approved_amount < requested_amount,
        have counteroffer_amount = approved_amount,
        have exposure_reduction_flag = TRUE,
        have approved PTI at or below counteroffer_target_pti,
        have approved Expected Loss at or below product_approved_el_cap,
        and have primary_reason_family = COUNTEROFFER.

Boundary:
    This query validates output conventions only.

    It does not recalculate approved exposure, approved PTI, approved Expected Loss, or reason codes.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
    ) AS final_counteroffer_without_feasibility_pass_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND (
                approved_amount IS NULL
             OR approved_amount <= 0
             OR approved_amount >= requested_amount
          )
    ) AS final_counteroffer_amount_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND counteroffer_amount IS DISTINCT FROM approved_amount
    ) AS final_counteroffer_amount_mismatch_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND exposure_reduction_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
    ) AS final_counteroffer_exposure_reduction_flag_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND approved_payment_to_income_ratio > counteroffer_target_pti
    ) AS final_counteroffer_approved_pti_above_target_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND approved_expected_loss_amount > product_approved_el_cap
    ) AS final_counteroffer_approved_el_above_product_cap_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND primary_reason_family <> 'COUNTEROFFER'
    ) AS final_counteroffer_reason_family_violation_rows

FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions;


/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.11F Counteroffer primary reason-code distribution.

Purpose:
    Explain why final COUNTEROFFER rows received reduced-exposure treatment.

Interpretation:
    Counteroffer reason codes may indicate:
        product exposure limits,
        affordability adjustment,
        approved Expected Loss limits,
        synthetic-risk exposure limits,

Boundary:

SELECT
    primary_reason_code,

    COUNT(*) AS final_counteroffer_rows,

    ROUND(
        100.0 * COUNT(*)::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (), 0),
        2
    ) AS pct_of_final_counteroffer_rows

FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions

WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'

GROUP BY primary_reason_code

ORDER BY
    final_counteroffer_rows DESC,
    primary_reason_code;

/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.12A Manual review summary.

Purpose:
    Provide the primary manual-review profile for latest-run QA.

Interpretation:
    This query shows:
        manual_review_rows,
        manual_review_rate,
        average synthetic estimated-risk proxy,
        average DTI,
        average requested PTI,
        average requested Expected Loss,
        failed-counteroffer-to-review volume,
        mixed-signal manual-review volume,

Documentation use:

SELECT
    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW') AS manual_review_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW')::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS manual_review_rate,

    AVG(estimated_pd) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW')
        AS avg_synthetic_estimated_pd_proxy_manual_review,

    AVG(debt_to_income_ratio) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW')
        AS avg_debt_to_income_ratio_manual_review,

    AVG(requested_payment_to_income_ratio) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW')
        AS avg_requested_payment_to_income_ratio_manual_review,

        AS avg_requested_expected_loss_amount_manual_review,

        WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
          AND preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS FALSE
    ) AS failed_counteroffer_to_manual_review_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
          AND mixed_signal_profile_type <> 'NO_MIXED_SIGNAL_PROFILE'
    ) AS mixed_signal_manual_review_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
          AND borderline_profile_flag IS TRUE
    ) AS borderline_manual_review_rows

    Provide the primary final no-offer profile for latest-run QA.

Interpretation:
    This query summarizes decline volume, decline rate, average risk / capacity profile, and major
    trigger-family counts among declined rows.

Documentation use:
    This is the headline decline / policy-stop QA table.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE') AS decline_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE')::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS decline_rate,

    AVG(estimated_pd) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE')
        AS avg_synthetic_estimated_pd_proxy_declines,

    AVG(debt_to_income_ratio) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE')

        AS avg_requested_payment_to_income_ratio_declines,

    AVG(requested_expected_loss_amount) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE')
        AS avg_requested_expected_loss_amount_declines,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
          AND hard_policy_stop_flag IS TRUE
    ) AS hard_policy_stop_decline_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
          AND synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
    ) AS synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_decline_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
          AND dti_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
    ) AS dti_decline_trigger_decline_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
          AND requested_pti_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
    ) AS requested_pti_decline_trigger_decline_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
          AND requested_el_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
    ) AS requested_el_decline_trigger_decline_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'

/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.14H Reason-code concentration diagnostic.

Purpose:
    Identify whether one primary reason code dominates the latest-run output.

Interpretation:
    REASON_CODE_CONCENTRATION_REVIEW_REQUIRED is a documentation prompt, not automatic invalidation.

    A dominant reason code can be valid if it is explained by:
        active strategy thresholds,
        product mix,
        score mix,
        affordability pressure,
        exposure limits,
        counteroffer settings,
        or scenario design.

Threshold boundary:
    The 0.500000 threshold is a QA review prompt only.

    It is not a production limit.
    It is not empirical model validation.
    It is not a policy acceptance threshold.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

WITH reason_distribution AS (
    SELECT
        primary_reason_code,
        COUNT(*) AS row_count,
        COUNT(*)::NUMERIC / NULLIF(SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (), 0)
            AS primary_reason_code_share

    FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions

    GROUP BY primary_reason_code
)

SELECT
    primary_reason_code,
    row_count,
    primary_reason_code_share,

    CASE
        WHEN primary_reason_code_share > 0.500000
            THEN 'REASON_CODE_CONCENTRATION_REVIEW_REQUIRED'
        ELSE 'REASON_CODE_CONCENTRATION_WITHIN_REVIEW_RANGE'
    END AS reason_code_concentration_status

FROM reason_distribution

ORDER BY
    primary_reason_code_share DESC,
    primary_reason_code;

    4. Review simulated offer-exposure profile by product.
    5. Review simulated offer-exposure profile by score band.

        COUNTEROFFER.

    MANUAL_REVIEW remains unresolved and should not be counted as positive offer exposure.

    DECLINE is a simulated no-offer outcome and should not be counted as positive offer exposure.

WHY THIS PROFILE IS SEPARATE FROM ALL-APPLICATION METRICS:
    All-application metrics show total portfolio impact.

    Offer-row metrics show the profile of rows where the simulated strategy assigned positive
    exposure.

    Both are useful, but they answer different questions.

TERMINOLOGY:

        simulated offer exposure,
        offer rows,
        positive offer outcomes.

EXPECTED LOSS BOUNDARY:

    treatment.

/*-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14.15A Overall simulated offer-exposure profile.

Purpose:
    Provide the headline simulated positive offer-exposure profile.

Interpretation:
    offer_count:
        APPROVE + COUNTEROFFER rows.

    approval_share_of_offers:
        share of offer rows resolved as APPROVE.

    counteroffer_share_of_offers:
        share of offer rows resolved as COUNTEROFFER.

    total_approved_amount_offer_rows:
        simulated approved exposure among positive offer-exposure rows only.

    total_approved_expected_loss_offer_rows:
        approved-exposure Expected Loss among positive offer-exposure rows only.

Boundary:
    These are simulated offer-exposure metrics only, not booked exposure or funded exposure.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS applications,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)
        AS offer_count,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS simulated_offer_exposure_rate,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'APPROVE')
        AS approval_offer_count,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
        AS counteroffer_offer_count,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'APPROVE')::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(
            COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE),
            0
        ) AS approval_share_of_offers,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')::NUMERIC
        / NULLIF(
            COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE),
            0
        ) AS counteroffer_share_of_offers,

    SUM(COALESCE(approved_amount, 0)) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)
        AS total_approved_amount_offer_rows,

    AVG(approved_amount) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)
        AS avg_approved_amount_offer_rows,

    AVG(estimated_pd) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)
        AS avg_synthetic_estimated_pd_proxy_offer_rows,

    AVG(estimated_lgd) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)
        AS avg_estimated_lgd_offer_rows,

    AVG(requested_payment_to_income_ratio) FILTER (WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag IS TRUE)
        AS avg_requested_pti_offer_rows,

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
    Confirm that locked final output conventions hold in the latest-run table.

    Section 14.17 answers:

        "Do final decision flags, simulated offer-exposure flags, approved amount conventions,
         affordability conventions, approved Expected Loss conventions, and reason-code conventions
         reconcile to final decision_outcome?"

SQL PURPOSE:
    1. Produce compact violation counts for final output conventions.
    2. Confirm exactly one final decision flag is TRUE per row.
    3. Confirm simulated_offer_exposure_flag reconciles to APPROVE and COUNTEROFFER.
    4. Confirm approved amount conventions by final outcome.
    5. Confirm approved monthly payment and approved PTI conventions by final outcome.
    6. Confirm approved Expected Loss conventions by final outcome.
    7. Confirm simulated reason-code completeness.
    8. Confirm simulated reason-family alignment.

EXPECTED RESULT:
    All violation counts should be zero.

WHY THIS SECTION IS READ-ONLY:
    The latest-run table was materialized in Section 11.

    Section 14 validates the output but does not repair, overwrite, or recalculate it.

INTERPRETATION:
    Non-zero violation counts should be reviewed before any Validation Summary, README, LinkedIn, or
    executive narrative claims are drafted.

    A convention violation is stronger evidence than an ordinary diagnostic REVIEW pattern.

    It suggests a potential implementation issue or a broken upstream assumption.

BOUNDARY:
    Section 14.17 does not modify latest-run rows.
    Section 14.17 does not modify archive rows.
    Section 14.17 does not recalculate decisions.
    Section 14.17 does not reassign reason codes.
    Section 14.17 does not recalculate approved exposure.
    Section 14.17 does not recalculate approved Expected Loss.

***************************************************************************************************/

SELECT
    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE (
            COALESCE(approval_flag::INT, 0)
            + COALESCE(counteroffer_flag::INT, 0)
            + COALESCE(manual_review_flag::INT, 0)
            + COALESCE(decline_flag::INT, 0)
        ) <> 1
    ) AS final_outcome_flag_count_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE simulated_offer_exposure_flag
              IS DISTINCT FROM (decision_outcome IN ('APPROVE', 'COUNTEROFFER'))
    ) AS simulated_offer_exposure_flag_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
          AND approved_amount IS DISTINCT FROM requested_amount
    ) AS approve_amount_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND (
                approved_amount IS NULL
             OR approved_amount <= 0
             OR approved_amount >= requested_amount
             OR counteroffer_amount IS DISTINCT FROM approved_amount
          )
    ) AS counteroffer_amount_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
          AND approved_amount IS NOT NULL
    ) AS manual_review_amount_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
          AND approved_amount IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
    ) AS decline_amount_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
          AND approved_monthly_payment_proxy IS DISTINCT FROM requested_monthly_payment_proxy
    ) AS approve_payment_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND (
                approved_monthly_payment_proxy IS NULL
             OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio IS NULL
             OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio > counteroffer_target_pti
          )
    ) AS counteroffer_affordability_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
          AND (
                approved_monthly_payment_proxy IS NOT NULL
             OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio IS NOT NULL
          )
    ) AS manual_review_affordability_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
          AND (
                approved_monthly_payment_proxy IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
             OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
          )
    ) AS decline_affordability_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
          AND approved_expected_loss_amount IS DISTINCT FROM requested_expected_loss_amount
    ) AS approve_expected_loss_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
          AND (
                approved_expected_loss_amount IS NULL
             OR approved_expected_loss_amount < 0
             OR approved_expected_loss_amount > requested_expected_loss_amount
             OR approved_expected_loss_amount > product_approved_el_cap
          )
    ) AS counteroffer_expected_loss_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
          AND approved_expected_loss_amount IS NOT NULL
    ) AS manual_review_expected_loss_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
          AND approved_expected_loss_amount IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
    ) AS decline_expected_loss_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE primary_reason_code IS NULL
           OR primary_reason_family IS NULL
           OR triggered_reason_code_list IS NULL
           OR reason_code_count IS NULL
           OR reason_code_count < 1
    ) AS reason_code_completeness_violation_rows,

    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE (
                decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
            AND primary_reason_family <> 'APPROVAL'
        )
        OR (
                decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
            AND primary_reason_family <> 'COUNTEROFFER'
        )
        OR (
                decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
            AND primary_reason_family <> 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
        )
        OR (
                decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
            AND primary_reason_family <> 'POLICY_STOP'
        )
    ) AS reason_family_alignment_violation_rows
FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions;

WITH latest_context AS (

        archive_run_flag,
        strategy_archive_reset_mode,

        strategy_set_name,
        strategy_name,
        strategy_version,

        module1_scenario_set_name,
        module1_scenario_name,
        population_id,

        COUNT(*) AS latest_run_row_count,
        COUNT(DISTINCT application_id) AS latest_run_distinct_application_id_count
    FROM credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions
    GROUP BY
        archive_run_flag,
        strategy_archive_reset_mode,
        strategy_set_name,
        strategy_name,
        strategy_version,
        module1_scenario_set_name,
        module1_scenario_name,

        lc.archive_run_flag,
        lc.strategy_archive_reset_mode,

        lc.strategy_name,
        lc.strategy_version,

        lc.module1_scenario_set_name,
        lc.module1_scenario_name,
        lc.population_id,

        lc.latest_run_row_count,
        lc.latest_run_distinct_application_id_count,

        COUNT(ar.*) AS current_archived_slice_row_count,
        COUNT(DISTINCT ar.application_id) AS current_archived_slice_distinct_application_id_count,

        MIN(ar.archive_inserted_ts) AS min_archive_inserted_ts,
        MAX(ar.archive_inserted_ts) AS max_archive_inserted_ts

    FROM latest_context lc

    LEFT JOIN credit_decisioning_sim.strategy_decisions_archive ar
        ON ar.strategy_set_name = lc.strategy_set_name
       AND ar.strategy_name = lc.strategy_name
       AND ar.strategy_version = lc.strategy_version

        lc.archive_run_flag,

        lc.strategy_set_name,
        lc.strategy_name,
        lc.strategy_version,
        lc.module1_scenario_set_name,
        lc.module1_scenario_name,
        lc.population_id,
        lc.latest_run_row_count,
        lc.latest_run_distinct_application_id_count

ORDER BY
