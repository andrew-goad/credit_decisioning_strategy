/***************************************************************************************************
SECTION 7 — PRELIMINARY DECISION ROUTING LAYER
****************************************************************************************************

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
    Convert overlapping Section 6 rule-trigger flags into one preliminary strategy route.

    Section 7 answers:

        "Based on current triggers, what path should this synthetic applicant be routed toward before
         approved exposure / counteroffer feasibility is calculated?"

    Section 7 does NOT answer:

        "What is the final simulated strategy outcome?"

SQL PURPOSE:
    1. Read from tmp_m2_stage_3_rule_flags.
    2. Preserve all prior source, lineage, strategy metadata, segmentation, threshold, and rule-trigger
       fields.
    3. Apply a transparent preliminary routing hierarchy.
    4. Create preliminary_decision_outcome, preliminary_decision_path, preliminary priority, and
       preliminary route reason family.
    5. Create preliminary-only outcome flags.
    6. Create routing flags indicating whether later counteroffer feasibility and approved exposure
       calculations are required.
    7. Validate routing completeness and hierarchy consistency.
    8. Confirm that final outcome fields, final flags, reason codes, and approved-exposure economics
       are not yet created.

WHY THIS IS PRELIMINARY ROUTING, NOT FINAL DECISIONING:
    Section 6 identifies which simulated policy conditions are triggered.
    Section 7 prioritizes those triggers into a preliminary route.

    Final decision_outcome is deferred because preliminary COUNTEROFFER rows require Section 8
    approved-exposure and feasibility testing before they can become final COUNTEROFFER outcomes.

WHY COUNTEROFFER FEASIBILITY CANNOT BE FINALIZED HERE:
    A preliminary COUNTEROFFER route only means:

        "This row should be tested for reduced-exposure feasibility."

    It does not mean:
        approved_amount exists,
        counteroffer_amount exists,
        approved payment exists,
        approved PTI exists,
        approved Expected Loss exists,
        or a viable counteroffer has been created.

WHY HARD SIMULATED POLICY STOPS OVERRIDE OTHER TRIGGERS:
    Severe simulated policy stop conditions are intentionally routed before affordability or exposure
    treatments because they represent high-priority strategy stops in the simulated hierarchy.

WHY SEVERE SYNTHETIC ESTIMATED-RISK IS NOT SOLVED BY EXPOSURE REDUCTION:
    Module 2 V1.0 does not create a post-decision risk model and does not recalculate estimated_pd.

    estimated_pd remains the Module 1 synthetic estimated-PD proxy input. Severe synthetic risk is
    treated as a preliminary no-offer route before counteroffer testing.

WHY SEVERE DTI IS TREATED AS EXISTING-OBLIGATION CAPACITY:
    DTI reflects existing obligations. It is not directly corrected by reducing the requested exposure
    in Module 2 V1.0.

WHY REQUESTED-STRUCTURE SEVERE STRESS MAY ROUTE TO REVIEW:
    Requested PTI, requested Expected Loss, and requested exposure stress may be addressable through
    exposure treatment in some cases, but Section 8 has not yet tested feasibility.

    If a row is not a clean counteroffer candidate and is not routed by a higher-priority simulated
    no-offer condition, unresolved severe requested-structure stress routes to preliminary review.

WHY CLEAN APPROVAL IS STILL PRELIMINARY:
    Even a clean approval candidate still needs final approved_amount, approved payment, approved PTI,
    and approved Expected Loss populated later.

WHY RESIDUAL CASES ROUTE TO REVIEW:
    Unclassified profiles should not silently become approvals or declines.

    Residual review is intentionally conservative and audit-readable.

REASON-CODE BOUNDARY:
    Section 7 does not assign simulated reason codes.

APPROVED-EXPOSURE BOUNDARY:
    Section 7 does not calculate approved_amount, counteroffer_amount, approved payment, approved PTI,
    or approved Expected Loss.

***************************************************************************************************/

/***************************************************************************************************
SECTION 7.2 — CREATE PRELIMINARY DECISION ROUTING TABLE
****************************************************************************************************

TABLE:
    tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing

GRAIN:
    One row per application_id for the selected Module 1 scenario / population and active Module 2
    strategy.

INPUT:
    tmp_m2_stage_3_rule_flags

OUTPUT:
    All Section 6 fields plus preliminary routing fields only.

***************************************************************************************************/

CREATE TEMP TABLE tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing
ON COMMIT PRESERVE ROWS
AS
WITH route_inputs AS (
    SELECT
        rf.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Severe requested-structure trigger.

        These are requested-structure stress indicators, not final decision outcomes.

        They may indicate unresolved affordability, requested Expected Loss, or exposure stress that
        should not silently become clean approval before approved-exposure logic exists.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        (
            rf.requested_pti_decline_trigger_flag
            OR rf.requested_el_decline_trigger_flag
            OR rf.requested_exposure_extreme_flag
            OR rf.exposure_policy_stop_candidate_flag
        ) AS severe_requested_structure_trigger_flag,

        (
            rf.requested_pti_decline_trigger_flag::INT
            + rf.requested_el_decline_trigger_flag::INT
            + rf.requested_exposure_extreme_flag::INT
            + rf.exposure_policy_stop_candidate_flag::INT
        ) AS severe_requested_structure_trigger_count

    FROM tmp_m2_stage_3_rule_flags rf
),

route_assignment AS (
    SELECT
        ri.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        preliminary_decision_outcome.

        Allowed values:
            APPROVE
            COUNTEROFFER
            MANUAL_REVIEW
            DECLINE

        These are preliminary routing values only.
        Final decision_outcome is created later after exposure treatment and counteroffer feasibility.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ri.hard_policy_stop_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'DECLINE'

            WHEN ri.synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'DECLINE'

            WHEN ri.dti_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'DECLINE'

            WHEN ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER'

            WHEN ri.severe_requested_structure_trigger_flag IS TRUE
             AND ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
                THEN 'MANUAL_REVIEW'

            WHEN ri.manual_review_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'MANUAL_REVIEW'

            WHEN ri.clean_approval_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'APPROVE'

            ELSE 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
        END AS preliminary_decision_outcome,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        preliminary_decision_path.

        This identifies the preliminary route selected by the hierarchy.
        It is not a final decision_path.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ri.hard_policy_stop_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'HARD_POLICY_STOP'

            WHEN ri.synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'SEVERE_SYNTHETIC_RISK'

            WHEN ri.dti_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'SEVERE_DTI_CAPACITY'

            WHEN ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_FEASIBILITY_CHECK'

            WHEN ri.severe_requested_structure_trigger_flag IS TRUE
             AND ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
                THEN 'REQUESTED_STRUCTURE_SEVERE_REVIEW'

            WHEN ri.manual_review_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'MANUAL_REVIEW_CANDIDATE'

            WHEN ri.clean_approval_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'CLEAN_APPROVAL_CANDIDATE'

            ELSE 'RESIDUAL_STRATEGY_REVIEW'
        END AS preliminary_decision_path,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        preliminary_decision_priority_rank.

        Lower rank means higher priority in the preliminary routing hierarchy.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ri.hard_policy_stop_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 1

            WHEN ri.synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 2

            WHEN ri.dti_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 3

            WHEN ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 4

            WHEN ri.severe_requested_structure_trigger_flag IS TRUE
             AND ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
                THEN 5

            WHEN ri.manual_review_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 6

            WHEN ri.clean_approval_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 7

            ELSE 8
        END AS preliminary_decision_priority_rank,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        preliminary_route_reason_family.

        This is a routing family label, not a simulated reason code.
        Simulated reason codes are assigned after final decision resolution.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ri.hard_policy_stop_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'HARD_POLICY_STOP'

            WHEN ri.synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'SYNTHETIC_ESTIMATED_RISK'

            WHEN ri.dti_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'DTI_CAPACITY'

            WHEN ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_FEASIBILITY'

            WHEN ri.severe_requested_structure_trigger_flag IS TRUE
             AND ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
                THEN 'REQUESTED_STRUCTURE_REVIEW'

            WHEN ri.manual_review_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'MANUAL_REVIEW'

            WHEN ri.clean_approval_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'CLEAN_APPROVAL'

            ELSE 'RESIDUAL_REVIEW'
        END AS preliminary_route_reason_family,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        preliminary_route_trigger_count.

        Diagnostic only.

        This count measures how many broad routing families are active before hierarchy is applied.
        Multiple overlapping routing signals can validly exist; Section 7 chooses one preliminary
        route using priority order.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        (
            ri.hard_policy_stop_flag::INT
            + ri.synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag::INT
            + ri.dti_decline_trigger_flag::INT
            + ri.counteroffer_candidate_flag::INT
            + ri.severe_requested_structure_trigger_flag::INT
            + ri.manual_review_candidate_flag::INT
            + ri.clean_approval_candidate_flag::INT
        ) AS preliminary_route_trigger_count

    FROM route_inputs ri
),

preliminary_flags AS (
    SELECT
        ra.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Preliminary outcome flags.

        These flags reconcile to preliminary_decision_outcome only.
        Final approval_flag, counteroffer_flag, manual_review_flag, and decline_flag are created
        later after exposure-treatment feasibility and final decision resolution.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        (ra.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE') AS preliminary_decline_flag,
        (ra.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER') AS preliminary_counteroffer_flag,
        (ra.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW') AS preliminary_manual_review_flag,
        (ra.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE') AS preliminary_approve_flag,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        Exposure-routing flags.

        COUNTEROFFER:
            requires counteroffer feasibility testing and approved exposure calculation later.

        APPROVE:
            requires approved exposure calculation later so approved_amount and approved Expected
            Loss can be populated.

        DECLINE / MANUAL_REVIEW:
            do not require approved exposure calculation at this stage.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        (ra.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
            AS requires_counteroffer_feasibility_flag,

        (ra.preliminary_decision_outcome IN ('APPROVE', 'COUNTEROFFER'))
            AS requires_approved_exposure_calculation_flag

    FROM route_assignment ra
)

SELECT *
FROM preliminary_flags;



/***************************************************************************************************
SECTION 7.3 — LOCAL FAIL-FAST CHECKS FOR PRELIMINARY DECISION ROUTING
****************************************************************************************************

PURPOSE:
    Validate the Section 7 preliminary routing layer before approved exposure and counteroffer
    feasibility are calculated.

These are local staging gatekeepers only.

They are not:
    final decision validation,
    reason-code validation,
    approved-exposure validation,
    archive validation,
    or full Module 2 QA.

***************************************************************************************************/

DO $$
DECLARE
    v_stage3_count BIGINT;
    v_stage4_count BIGINT;
    v_stage4_distinct_app_count BIGINT;
    v_duplicate_application_count BIGINT;

    v_preliminary_completeness_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_preliminary_flag_reconciliation_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_priority_hierarchy_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_feasibility_flag_failure_count BIGINT;

    v_forbidden_column_count BIGINT;
BEGIN
    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: row count must be unchanged from Section 6 to Section 7.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_stage3_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_3_rule_flags;

    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_stage4_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing;

    IF v_stage4_count <> v_stage3_count THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 7 failure: row count changed from Section 6 to Section 7. Stage 3 rows: %, Stage 4 rows: %.',
            v_stage3_count,
            v_stage4_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: application_id remains unique.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT application_id)
    INTO v_stage4_distinct_app_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing;

    IF v_stage4_distinct_app_count <> v_stage4_count THEN
        SELECT COUNT(*)
        INTO v_duplicate_application_count
        FROM (
            SELECT application_id
            FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing
            GROUP BY application_id
            HAVING COUNT(*) > 1
        ) d;

        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 7 failure: application_id uniqueness was not preserved. Stage 4 rows: %, distinct application_id count: %, duplicate application_id group count: %.',
            v_stage4_count,
            v_stage4_distinct_app_count,
            v_duplicate_application_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: preliminary routing fields must be complete and valid.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_preliminary_completeness_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing
    WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome IS NULL
       OR preliminary_decision_outcome NOT IN ('APPROVE', 'COUNTEROFFER', 'MANUAL_REVIEW', 'DECLINE')
       OR preliminary_decision_path IS NULL
       OR NULLIF(BTRIM(preliminary_decision_path), '') IS NULL
       OR preliminary_decision_priority_rank IS NULL
       OR preliminary_decision_priority_rank <= 0
       OR preliminary_route_reason_family IS NULL
       OR NULLIF(BTRIM(preliminary_route_reason_family), '') IS NULL
       OR preliminary_route_trigger_count IS NULL
       OR preliminary_route_trigger_count < 0
       OR severe_requested_structure_trigger_flag IS NULL
       OR severe_requested_structure_trigger_count IS NULL
       OR severe_requested_structure_trigger_count < 0;

    IF v_preliminary_completeness_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 7 failure: % row(s) have null or invalid preliminary routing fields.',
            v_preliminary_completeness_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: exactly one preliminary outcome flag must be TRUE per row and reconcile to
    preliminary_decision_outcome.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_preliminary_flag_reconciliation_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing
    WHERE (
            COALESCE(preliminary_decline_flag::INT, 0)
            + COALESCE(preliminary_counteroffer_flag::INT, 0)
            + COALESCE(preliminary_manual_review_flag::INT, 0)
            + COALESCE(preliminary_approve_flag::INT, 0)
          ) <> 1
       OR preliminary_decline_flag
          IS DISTINCT FROM (preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE')
       OR preliminary_counteroffer_flag
          IS DISTINCT FROM (preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
       OR preliminary_manual_review_flag
          IS DISTINCT FROM (preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW')
       OR preliminary_approve_flag
          IS DISTINCT FROM (preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE');

    IF v_preliminary_flag_reconciliation_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 7 failure: % row(s) have preliminary flag reconciliation failures.',
            v_preliminary_flag_reconciliation_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: priority hierarchy enforcement.

    This validates the accepted preliminary routing hierarchy without forcing mutual exclusivity
    across underlying rule-trigger families.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_priority_hierarchy_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing
    WHERE
        /* Priority 1 */
        (
            hard_policy_stop_flag IS TRUE
            AND NOT (
                preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                AND preliminary_decision_path = 'HARD_POLICY_STOP'
                AND preliminary_decision_priority_rank = 1
            )
        )

        OR

        /* Priority 2 */
        (
            hard_policy_stop_flag IS FALSE
            AND synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
            AND NOT (
                preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                AND preliminary_decision_path = 'SEVERE_SYNTHETIC_RISK'
                AND preliminary_decision_priority_rank = 2
            )
        )

        OR

        /* Priority 3 */
        (
            hard_policy_stop_flag IS FALSE
            AND synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS FALSE
            AND dti_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
            AND NOT (
                preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                AND preliminary_decision_path = 'SEVERE_DTI_CAPACITY'
                AND preliminary_decision_priority_rank = 3
            )
        )

        OR

        /* Priority 4 */
        (
            hard_policy_stop_flag IS FALSE
            AND synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS FALSE
            AND dti_decline_trigger_flag IS FALSE
            AND counteroffer_candidate_flag IS TRUE
            AND NOT (
                preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
                AND preliminary_decision_path = 'COUNTEROFFER_FEASIBILITY_CHECK'
                AND preliminary_decision_priority_rank = 4
            )
        )

        OR

        /* Preliminary COUNTEROFFER requires counteroffer candidate flag. */
        (
            preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
            AND counteroffer_candidate_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
        )

        OR

        /* Preliminary APPROVE requires clean approval candidate flag. */
        (
            preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
            AND clean_approval_candidate_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
        )

        OR

        /* Residual review only when no higher-priority route applies. */
        (
            preliminary_decision_path = 'RESIDUAL_STRATEGY_REVIEW'
            AND (
                hard_policy_stop_flag IS TRUE
                OR synthetic_risk_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                OR dti_decline_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                OR counteroffer_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                OR severe_requested_structure_trigger_flag IS TRUE
                OR manual_review_candidate_flag IS TRUE
                OR clean_approval_candidate_flag IS TRUE
            )
        );

    IF v_priority_hierarchy_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 7 failure: % row(s) violate preliminary routing hierarchy.',
            v_priority_hierarchy_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: feasibility-routing flags must reconcile to preliminary_decision_outcome.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_feasibility_flag_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing
    WHERE (
            preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
            AND requires_counteroffer_feasibility_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
    )
    OR (
            preliminary_decision_outcome IN ('APPROVE', 'COUNTEROFFER')
            AND requires_approved_exposure_calculation_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
    )
    OR (
            preliminary_decision_outcome IN ('DECLINE', 'MANUAL_REVIEW')
            AND requires_approved_exposure_calculation_flag IS DISTINCT FROM FALSE
    )
    OR (
            preliminary_decision_outcome <> 'COUNTEROFFER'
            AND requires_counteroffer_feasibility_flag IS DISTINCT FROM FALSE
    );

    IF v_feasibility_flag_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 7 failure: % row(s) have exposure / counteroffer feasibility routing flag failures.',
            v_feasibility_flag_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: Section 7 must not create final decision, reason-code, or approved-exposure fields.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_forbidden_column_count
    FROM pg_attribute
    WHERE attrelid = 'tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing'::REGCLASS
      AND attnum > 0
      AND NOT attisdropped
      AND attname IN (
            'decision_outcome',
            'decision_path',
            'approval_flag',
            'decline_flag',
            'manual_review_flag',
            'counteroffer_flag',
            'simulated_offer_exposure_flag',

            'primary_reason_code',
            'secondary_reason_code',
            'tertiary_reason_code',
            'triggered_reason_code_list',
            'reason_priority_rank',
            'primary_reason_family',

            'approved_amount',
            'counteroffer_amount',
            'approved_monthly_payment_proxy',
            'approved_payment_to_income_ratio',
            'approved_expected_loss_amount'
      );

    IF v_forbidden_column_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 7 failure: forbidden final decision, reason-code, or approved-exposure field(s) were created in preliminary routing stage.';
    END IF;
END $$;

7.4.1 Count by preliminary_decision_outcome.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

    preliminary_decision_outcome,
    COUNT(*) AS row_count,
    ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(*)::NUMERIC / NULLIF(SUM(COUNT(*)) OVER (), 0), 2) AS pct_of_stage
FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing
GROUP BY preliminary_decision_outcome
ORDER BY
    CASE preliminary_decision_outcome
        WHEN 'APPROVE' THEN 1
        WHEN 'COUNTEROFFER' THEN 2
        WHEN 'MANUAL_REVIEW' THEN 3
        WHEN 'DECLINE' THEN 4
        ELSE 99
    END;

SECTION 8 — APPROVED EXPOSURE AND COUNTEROFFER FEASIBILITY LAYER
****************************************************************************************************

BUSINESS PURPOSE:
    Calculate preliminary approved-exposure economics and counteroffer feasibility after preliminary
    routing has identified which rows require exposure treatment.

    Section 8 answers:

        "Given the preliminary route, what approved exposure treatment would be economically and
         structurally feasible?"

    Section 8 does NOT answer:

        "What is the final simulated strategy outcome?"

SQL PURPOSE:
    1. Read from tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing.
    2. Preserve all prior lineage, strategy, segmentation, rule-trigger, and preliminary routing fields.
    3. Calculate preliminary approved exposure treatment by preliminary route.
    4. Evaluate reduced-exposure counteroffer feasibility for preliminary COUNTEROFFER rows.
    5. Calculate preliminary approved payment, approved PTI, and approved Expected Loss only where
       appropriate.
    6. Preserve failed counteroffers as unresolved for later final decision resolution.
    7. Validate exposure-treatment conventions and feasibility logic.
    8. Confirm that final decision fields and simulated reason codes are not created yet.

WHY APPROVED EXPOSURE TREATMENT IS SEPARATED FROM PRELIMINARY ROUTING:
    Preliminary routing identifies the path.
    Section 8 tests the economic and structural feasibility of exposure treatment.

    This separation allows counteroffer feasibility to be transparent and auditable.

WHY PRELIMINARY COUNTEROFFER DOES NOT GUARANTEE A VIABLE COUNTEROFFER:
    A preliminary COUNTEROFFER route only means the row requires reduced-exposure feasibility testing.

    A viable counteroffer must satisfy:
        product cap,
        product minimum,
        minimum counteroffer percent,
        maximum exposure reduction,
        approved PTI target,
        approved Expected Loss cap.

WHY APPROVED AMOUNT IS STILL PRELIMINARY:
    Section 8 calculates preliminary approved exposure treatment.
    Section 9 resolves final decision_outcome and final exposure fields.

WHY APPROVED PAYMENT AND APPROVED PTI ARE STRATEGY-SIMULATION PROXIES:
    approved_monthly_payment_proxy_preliminary scales the requested monthly payment proxy
    proportionally with approved exposure.

    This is not a contractual amortization schedule.

WHY APPROVED EXPECTED LOSS IS BASED ON APPROVED EXPOSURE:
    requested_expected_loss_amount uses requested exposure from Module 1.
    approved_expected_loss_amount_preliminary uses preliminary approved exposure from Module 2.

WHY ESTIMATED_PD IS NOT RECALCULATED:
    estimated_pd remains the finalized Module 1 SQL field and is interpreted as the synthetic
    estimated-PD proxy.

    Module 2 V1.0 does not create a post-decision risk model.

WHY FAILED COUNTEROFFERS ARE NOT RESOLVED HERE:
    Failed counteroffers require a governed final-resolution policy:
        MANUAL_REVIEW
        or DECLINE

    That resolution occurs in Section 9.

MANUAL REVIEW BOUNDARY:
    Manual review remains unresolved by design.

DECLINE BOUNDARY:
    Preliminary DECLINE rows receive the locked zero-exposure convention here, but final DECLINE is
    still not assigned until Section 9.

REASON-CODE BOUNDARY:
    Section 8 does not assign simulated reason codes.

FINAL OUTCOME BOUNDARY:
    Section 8 does not create final decision_outcome, final decision_path, final decision flags,
    simulated_offer_exposure_flag, or final latest-run output.

    affordability fields, preliminary approved economics fields, and counteroffer feasibility
    diagnostics.

***************************************************************************************************/

CREATE TEMP TABLE tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment
ON COMMIT PRESERVE ROWS
AS
WITH exposure_inputs AS (
    SELECT
        r.*
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing r
),

counteroffer_components AS (
    SELECT
        ei.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Product cap amount.

        The approved exposure cannot exceed either:
            original requested_amount
            product_max_approved_amount
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ei.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
                THEN LEAST(ei.requested_amount, ei.product_max_approved_amount)
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_product_cap_amount,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        PTI target amount.

        Because approved payment is scaled proportionally in Module 2 V1.0, this estimates the
        approved amount that would bring approved PTI to the product-specific target.

        This remains a strategy-simulation proxy, not a contractual payment schedule.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ei.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND ei.requested_payment_to_income_ratio > 0
                THEN ei.requested_amount
                     * ei.counteroffer_target_pti
                     / NULLIF(ei.requested_payment_to_income_ratio, 0)

            WHEN ei.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
                THEN ei.requested_amount

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_pti_target_amount,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        Approved Expected Loss cap amount.

        This is the maximum exposure that can satisfy the approved-exposure Expected Loss cap.

        Formula:
            product_approved_el_cap / NULLIF(estimated_pd × estimated_lgd, 0)

        estimated_pd is interpreted as the synthetic estimated-PD proxy.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ei.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
                THEN ei.product_approved_el_cap / NULLIF((ei.estimated_pd * ei.estimated_lgd), 0)
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_el_cap_amount,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        Minimum viable counteroffer amount.

        The counteroffer must not fall below:
            product minimum approved amount,
            minimum viable percentage of requested amount,
            floor implied by maximum permitted exposure reduction.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ei.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
                THEN GREATEST(
                    ei.product_min_approved_amount,
                    ei.requested_amount * ei.product_min_counteroffer_pct,
                    ei.requested_amount * (1.0 - ei.product_max_exposure_reduction_pct)
                )
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_min_viable_amount

    FROM exposure_inputs ei
),

counteroffer_raw AS (
    SELECT
        cc.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Raw maximum feasible counteroffer amount.

        The raw maximum feasible amount is the most conservative of:
            requested amount,
            product cap amount,
            PTI target amount,
            approved Expected Loss cap amount.

        If any key component is unavailable, the raw amount is NULL so feasibility fails explicitly.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN cc.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cc.counteroffer_product_cap_amount IS NOT NULL
             AND cc.counteroffer_pti_target_amount IS NOT NULL
             AND cc.counteroffer_el_cap_amount IS NOT NULL
                THEN LEAST(
                    cc.requested_amount,
                    cc.counteroffer_product_cap_amount,
                    cc.counteroffer_pti_target_amount,
                    cc.counteroffer_el_cap_amount
                )
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_raw_max_feasible_amount

    FROM counteroffer_components cc
),

counteroffer_rounding AS (
    SELECT
        cr.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Conservative counteroffer rounding.

        Rounding down ensures the rounded amount does not accidentally exceed:
            product cap,
            PTI target,
            approved Expected Loss cap.

        If rounding pushes the amount below the minimum viable amount, feasibility fails later.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN cr.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cr.counteroffer_raw_max_feasible_amount IS NOT NULL
             AND cr.approved_amount_rounding_increment > 0
                THEN FLOOR(cr.counteroffer_raw_max_feasible_amount / cr.approved_amount_rounding_increment)
                     * cr.approved_amount_rounding_increment
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_rounded_amount

    FROM counteroffer_raw cr
),

counteroffer_test_economics AS (
    SELECT
        cor.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Candidate counteroffer approved payment proxy.

        This value is calculated for feasibility testing only.

        If feasibility fails, the final preliminary approved payment fields are set to NULL for the
        failed preliminary COUNTEROFFER route.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN cor.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cor.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                THEN cor.requested_monthly_payment_proxy
                     * cor.counteroffer_rounded_amount
                     / NULLIF(cor.requested_amount, 0)
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_test_monthly_payment_proxy,

        CASE
            WHEN cor.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cor.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                THEN (
                    cor.requested_monthly_payment_proxy
                    * cor.counteroffer_rounded_amount
                    / NULLIF(cor.requested_amount, 0)
                ) / NULLIF((cor.annual_income / 12.0), 0)
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_test_payment_to_income_ratio,

        CASE
            WHEN cor.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cor.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                THEN cor.counteroffer_rounded_amount * cor.estimated_pd * cor.estimated_lgd
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount

    FROM counteroffer_rounding cor
),

counteroffer_feasibility AS (
    SELECT
        cte.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Counteroffer feasibility constraint count.

        Diagnostic only.

        Counts failed constraints among preliminary COUNTEROFFER rows. Non-counteroffer preliminary
        routes receive NULL.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN cte.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER' THEN
                (
                    (cte.counteroffer_allowed_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE)::INT
                    + (cte.counteroffer_raw_max_feasible_amount IS NULL)::INT
                    + (cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NULL)::INT
                    + (
                        cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                        AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount <= 0
                      )::INT
                    + (
                        cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                        AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount >= cte.requested_amount
                      )::INT
                    + (
                        cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                        AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount < cte.product_min_approved_amount
                      )::INT
                    + (
                        cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                        AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount < cte.requested_amount * cte.product_min_counteroffer_pct
                      )::INT
                    + (
                        cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                        AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount < cte.requested_amount * (1.0 - cte.product_max_exposure_reduction_pct)
                      )::INT
                    + (
                        cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                        AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount > cte.product_max_approved_amount
                      )::INT
                    + (
                        cte.counteroffer_test_payment_to_income_ratio IS NOT NULL
                        AND cte.counteroffer_test_payment_to_income_ratio > cte.counteroffer_target_pti
                      )::INT
                    + (
                        cte.counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount IS NOT NULL
                        AND cte.counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount > cte.product_approved_el_cap
                      )::INT
                )
            ELSE NULL::INTEGER
        END AS counteroffer_feasibility_constraint_count,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        Counteroffer feasibility pass flag.

        A preliminary COUNTEROFFER row passes only when every required feasibility condition is met.
        Non-counteroffer routes receive NULL.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN cte.preliminary_decision_outcome <> 'COUNTEROFFER'
                THEN NULL::BOOLEAN

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_allowed_flag IS TRUE
             AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
             AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount > 0
             AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount < cte.requested_amount
             AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount >= cte.product_min_approved_amount
             AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount >= cte.requested_amount * cte.product_min_counteroffer_pct
             AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount >= cte.requested_amount * (1.0 - cte.product_max_exposure_reduction_pct)
             AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount <= cte.product_max_approved_amount
             AND cte.counteroffer_test_payment_to_income_ratio <= cte.counteroffer_target_pti
             AND cte.counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount <= cte.product_approved_el_cap
                THEN TRUE

            ELSE FALSE
        END AS counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        Counteroffer feasibility failure reason.

        Deterministic priority order.

        These are feasibility diagnostics, not final simulated reason codes.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN cte.preliminary_decision_outcome <> 'COUNTEROFFER'
                THEN NULL::TEXT

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_allowed_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_NOT_ALLOWED_BY_PRODUCT'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_raw_max_feasible_amount IS NULL
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_RAW_AMOUNT_NULL'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NULL
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_ROUNDED_AMOUNT_NULL'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount <= 0
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_ROUNDED_AMOUNT_NONPOSITIVE'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount >= cte.requested_amount
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_NOT_BELOW_REQUESTED_AMOUNT'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount < cte.product_min_approved_amount
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_BELOW_PRODUCT_MINIMUM'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount < cte.requested_amount * cte.product_min_counteroffer_pct
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_BELOW_MINIMUM_REQUESTED_PERCENT'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount < cte.requested_amount * (1.0 - cte.product_max_exposure_reduction_pct)
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_EXCEEDS_MAX_REDUCTION'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount > cte.product_max_approved_amount
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_EXCEEDS_PRODUCT_CAP'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_test_payment_to_income_ratio > cte.counteroffer_target_pti
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_PTI_STILL_ABOVE_TARGET'

            WHEN cte.counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount > cte.product_approved_el_cap
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_APPROVED_EL_ABOVE_CAP'

            WHEN NOT (
                cte.counteroffer_allowed_flag IS TRUE
                AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount IS NOT NULL
                AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount > 0
                AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount < cte.requested_amount
                AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount >= cte.product_min_approved_amount
                AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount >= cte.requested_amount * cte.product_min_counteroffer_pct
                AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount >= cte.requested_amount * (1.0 - cte.product_max_exposure_reduction_pct)
                AND cte.counteroffer_rounded_amount <= cte.product_max_approved_amount
                AND cte.counteroffer_test_payment_to_income_ratio <= cte.counteroffer_target_pti
                AND cte.counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount <= cte.product_approved_el_cap
            )
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_FEASIBILITY_UNKNOWN'

            ELSE NULL::TEXT
        END AS counteroffer_feasibility_failure_reason

    FROM counteroffer_test_economics cte
),

exposure_amount_assignment AS (
    SELECT
        cf.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        approved_amount_preliminary.

        APPROVE:
            requested_amount

        COUNTEROFFER:
            rounded feasible reduced exposure when feasibility passes

        DECLINE:
            zero preliminary exposure convention

        MANUAL_REVIEW:
            NULL, unresolved by design
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN cf.requested_amount

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cf.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN cf.counteroffer_rounded_amount

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN 0::NUMERIC

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN NULL::NUMERIC

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS approved_amount_preliminary,

        CASE
            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cf.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN cf.counteroffer_rounded_amount
            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS counteroffer_amount_preliminary,

        CASE
            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cf.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN TRUE
            ELSE FALSE
        END AS exposure_reduction_flag_preliminary,

        CASE
            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN 0::NUMERIC

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cf.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN cf.requested_amount - cf.counteroffer_rounded_amount

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN cf.requested_amount

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN NULL::NUMERIC

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS exposure_reduction_amount_preliminary,

        CASE
            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN 0::NUMERIC

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND cf.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN (cf.requested_amount - cf.counteroffer_rounded_amount)
                     / NULLIF(cf.requested_amount, 0)

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN 1.0::NUMERIC

            WHEN cf.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN NULL::NUMERIC

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS exposure_reduction_pct_preliminary

    FROM counteroffer_feasibility cf
),

approved_affordability AS (
    SELECT
        eaa.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Preliminary approved payment and approved PTI.

        APPROVE:
            preserve requested payment and requested PTI.

        COUNTEROFFER:
            use feasible counteroffer economics.

        DECLINE:
            zero preliminary exposure convention.

        MANUAL_REVIEW:
            unresolved NULL convention.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN eaa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN eaa.requested_monthly_payment_proxy

            WHEN eaa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND eaa.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN eaa.counteroffer_test_monthly_payment_proxy

            WHEN eaa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN 0::NUMERIC

            WHEN eaa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN NULL::NUMERIC

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS approved_monthly_payment_proxy_preliminary,

        CASE
            WHEN eaa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN eaa.requested_payment_to_income_ratio

            WHEN eaa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND eaa.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN eaa.counteroffer_test_payment_to_income_ratio

            WHEN eaa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN 0::NUMERIC

            WHEN eaa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN NULL::NUMERIC

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS approved_payment_to_income_ratio_preliminary

    FROM exposure_amount_assignment eaa
),

approved_economics AS (
    SELECT
        aa.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Preliminary approved Expected Loss.

        APPROVE:
            requested_expected_loss_amount

        COUNTEROFFER:
            rounded approved exposure × estimated_pd × estimated_lgd

        DECLINE:
            zero preliminary exposure convention

        MANUAL_REVIEW:
            unresolved NULL convention
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN aa.requested_expected_loss_amount

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND aa.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN aa.counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN 0::NUMERIC

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN NULL::NUMERIC

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS approved_expected_loss_amount_preliminary,

        CASE
            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN 0::NUMERIC

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND aa.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN aa.requested_expected_loss_amount - aa.counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN aa.requested_expected_loss_amount

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN NULL::NUMERIC

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS approved_el_reduction_amount_preliminary,

        CASE
            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN 0::NUMERIC

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND aa.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN (aa.requested_expected_loss_amount - aa.counteroffer_test_expected_loss_amount)
                     / NULLIF(aa.requested_expected_loss_amount, 0)

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN CASE
                    WHEN aa.requested_expected_loss_amount > 0 THEN 1.0::NUMERIC
                    ELSE 0::NUMERIC
                END

            WHEN aa.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN NULL::NUMERIC

            ELSE NULL::NUMERIC
        END AS approved_el_reduction_pct_preliminary

    FROM approved_affordability aa
),

final_exposure_treatment AS (
    SELECT
        ae.*,

        /*******************************************************************************************
        Counteroffer feasibility evaluated flag.

        Only preliminary COUNTEROFFER rows receive feasibility evaluation.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        (ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER')
            AS counteroffer_feasibility_evaluated_flag,


        /*******************************************************************************************
        exposure_treatment_status and exposure_treatment_path.

        These are preliminary exposure-treatment labels.
        They are not final decision outcomes and not simulated reason codes.
        *******************************************************************************************/
        CASE
            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN 'APPROVED_AS_REQUESTED_PRELIMINARY'

            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN 'NO_OFFER_PRELIMINARY_DECLINE'

            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN 'UNRESOLVED_MANUAL_REVIEW'

            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND ae.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_FEASIBLE_PRELIMINARY'

            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND ae.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS FALSE
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_NOT_FEASIBLE_PRELIMINARY'

            ELSE 'EXPOSURE_TREATMENT_UNKNOWN'
        END AS exposure_treatment_status,

        CASE
            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
                THEN 'APPROVE_AS_REQUESTED'

            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
                THEN 'NO_OFFER'

            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
                THEN 'MANUAL_REVIEW_UNRESOLVED'

            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND ae.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS TRUE
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_FEASIBLE'

            WHEN ae.preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
             AND ae.counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS FALSE
                THEN 'COUNTEROFFER_NOT_FEASIBLE'

            ELSE 'EXPOSURE_TREATMENT_UNKNOWN'
        END AS exposure_treatment_path

    FROM approved_economics ae
)

SELECT *
FROM final_exposure_treatment;



/***************************************************************************************************
SECTION 8.3 — LOCAL FAIL-FAST CHECKS FOR EXPOSURE TREATMENT
****************************************************************************************************

PURPOSE:
    Validate the Section 8 preliminary exposure-treatment layer before final decision resolution.

These are local staging gatekeepers only.

They are not:
    final decision validation,
    simulated reason-code validation,
    archive validation,
    or full Module 2 QA.

***************************************************************************************************/

DO $$
DECLARE
    v_stage4_count BIGINT;
    v_stage5_count BIGINT;
    v_stage5_distinct_app_count BIGINT;
    v_duplicate_application_count BIGINT;

    v_required_field_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_approve_route_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_decline_route_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_manual_review_route_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_counteroffer_route_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_counteroffer_pass_failure_count BIGINT;
    v_counteroffer_fail_failure_count BIGINT;

    v_forbidden_column_count BIGINT;
BEGIN
    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: row count must be unchanged from Section 7 to Section 8.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_stage4_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_4_preliminary_decision_routing;

    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_stage5_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment;

    IF v_stage5_count <> v_stage4_count THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: row count changed from Section 7 to Section 8. Stage 4 rows: %, Stage 5 rows: %.',
            v_stage4_count,
            v_stage5_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: application_id remains unique.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT application_id)
    INTO v_stage5_distinct_app_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment;

    IF v_stage5_distinct_app_count <> v_stage5_count THEN
        SELECT COUNT(*)
        INTO v_duplicate_application_count
        FROM (
            SELECT application_id
            FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment
            GROUP BY application_id
            HAVING COUNT(*) > 1
        ) d;

        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: application_id uniqueness was not preserved. Stage 5 rows: %, distinct application_id count: %, duplicate application_id group count: %.',
            v_stage5_count,
            v_stage5_distinct_app_count,
            v_duplicate_application_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: required exposure-treatment fields must be complete.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_required_field_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment
    WHERE exposure_treatment_status IS NULL
       OR NULLIF(BTRIM(exposure_treatment_status), '') IS NULL
       OR exposure_treatment_path IS NULL
       OR NULLIF(BTRIM(exposure_treatment_path), '') IS NULL
       OR counteroffer_feasibility_evaluated_flag IS NULL;

    IF v_required_field_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: % row(s) have missing exposure-treatment status/path or feasibility-evaluated flag.',
            v_required_field_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: preliminary APPROVE route convention.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_approve_route_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment
    WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'APPROVE'
      AND (
            approved_amount_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM requested_amount
         OR counteroffer_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_reduction_flag_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM FALSE
         OR exposure_reduction_amount_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
         OR exposure_reduction_pct_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
         OR approved_monthly_payment_proxy_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM requested_monthly_payment_proxy
         OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM requested_payment_to_income_ratio
         OR approved_expected_loss_amount_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM requested_expected_loss_amount
         OR approved_el_reduction_amount_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
         OR approved_el_reduction_pct_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
         OR counteroffer_feasibility_evaluated_flag IS DISTINCT FROM FALSE
         OR counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_treatment_status <> 'APPROVED_AS_REQUESTED_PRELIMINARY'
         OR exposure_treatment_path <> 'APPROVE_AS_REQUESTED'
      );

    IF v_approve_route_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: % preliminary APPROVE row(s) violate exposure convention.',
            v_approve_route_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: preliminary DECLINE route convention.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_decline_route_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment
    WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'DECLINE'
      AND (
            approved_amount_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
         OR counteroffer_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_reduction_flag_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM FALSE
         OR exposure_reduction_amount_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM requested_amount
         OR exposure_reduction_pct_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 1.0::NUMERIC
         OR approved_monthly_payment_proxy_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
         OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
         OR approved_expected_loss_amount_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM 0::NUMERIC
         OR approved_el_reduction_amount_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM requested_expected_loss_amount
         OR approved_el_reduction_pct_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM CASE
                WHEN requested_expected_loss_amount > 0 THEN 1.0::NUMERIC
                ELSE 0::NUMERIC
            END
         OR counteroffer_feasibility_evaluated_flag IS DISTINCT FROM FALSE
         OR counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_treatment_status <> 'NO_OFFER_PRELIMINARY_DECLINE'
         OR exposure_treatment_path <> 'NO_OFFER'
      );

    IF v_decline_route_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: % preliminary DECLINE row(s) violate zero-exposure convention.',
            v_decline_route_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: preliminary MANUAL_REVIEW route convention.

    Manual review remains unresolved by design.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_manual_review_route_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment
    WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'MANUAL_REVIEW'
      AND (
            approved_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR counteroffer_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_reduction_flag_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM FALSE
         OR exposure_reduction_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_reduction_pct_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_monthly_payment_proxy_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_expected_loss_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_el_reduction_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_el_reduction_pct_preliminary IS NOT NULL

    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

    INTO v_counteroffer_route_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment
    WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
      AND (
            counteroffer_feasibility_evaluated_flag IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
         OR counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS NULL
      );

    IF v_counteroffer_route_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: % preliminary COUNTEROFFER row(s) do not have feasibility evaluation populated.',

    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/

            approved_amount_preliminary IS NULL

         OR approved_amount_preliminary <= 0
         OR approved_amount_preliminary >= requested_amount
         OR exposure_reduction_flag_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM TRUE
         OR exposure_reduction_amount_preliminary <= 0
         OR exposure_reduction_pct_preliminary <= 0
         OR approved_amount_preliminary < product_min_approved_amount
         OR approved_amount_preliminary > product_max_approved_amount
         OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio_preliminary > counteroffer_target_pti
         OR approved_expected_loss_amount_preliminary > product_approved_el_cap
         OR counteroffer_feasibility_failure_reason IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_treatment_status <> 'COUNTEROFFER_FEASIBLE_PRELIMINARY'
         OR exposure_treatment_path <> 'COUNTEROFFER_FEASIBLE'
      );

    IF v_counteroffer_pass_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: % feasible preliminary COUNTEROFFER row(s) violate counteroffer feasibility convention.',
            v_counteroffer_pass_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: infeasible preliminary COUNTEROFFER convention.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_counteroffer_fail_failure_count
    FROM tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment
    WHERE preliminary_decision_outcome = 'COUNTEROFFER'
      AND counteroffer_feasibility_pass_flag IS FALSE
      AND (
            approved_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR counteroffer_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_reduction_flag_preliminary IS DISTINCT FROM FALSE
         OR exposure_reduction_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR exposure_reduction_pct_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_monthly_payment_proxy_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_payment_to_income_ratio_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_expected_loss_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_el_reduction_amount_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR approved_el_reduction_pct_preliminary IS NOT NULL
         OR counteroffer_feasibility_failure_reason IS NULL
         OR exposure_treatment_status <> 'COUNTEROFFER_NOT_FEASIBLE_PRELIMINARY'
         OR exposure_treatment_path <> 'COUNTEROFFER_NOT_FEASIBLE'
      );

    IF v_counteroffer_fail_failure_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: % infeasible preliminary COUNTEROFFER row(s) violate failed-counteroffer convention.',
            v_counteroffer_fail_failure_count;
    END IF;


    /*-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Gatekeeper: Section 8 must not create final decision, final flags, reason-code fields, or final
    output / archive logic.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------*/
    SELECT COUNT(*)
    INTO v_forbidden_column_count
    FROM pg_attribute
    WHERE attrelid = 'tmp_m2_stage_5_exposure_treatment'::REGCLASS
      AND attnum > 0
      AND NOT attisdropped
      AND attname IN (
            'decision_outcome',
            'decision_path',
            'approval_flag',
            'decline_flag',
            'manual_review_flag',
            'counteroffer_flag',
            'simulated_offer_exposure_flag',

            'primary_reason_code',
            'secondary_reason_code',
            'tertiary_reason_code',
            'triggered_reason_code_list',
            'reason_priority_rank',
            'primary_reason_family',

            'approved_amount',
            'counteroffer_amount',
            'approved_monthly_payment_proxy',
            'approved_payment_to_income_ratio',
            'approved_expected_loss_amount'
      );

    IF v_forbidden_column_count > 0 THEN
        RAISE EXCEPTION
            'Module 2 Section 8 failure: forbidden final decision, final exposure, or reason-code field(s) were created in exposure-treatment stage.';
    END IF;
END $$;
