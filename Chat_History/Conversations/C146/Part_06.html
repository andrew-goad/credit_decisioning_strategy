for experian 5300 expert compliance risk advisor remote role, must be 4k characters or less and cannot have ; character. Dear Experian Hiring Team,

I build analytical products around the people who use them: what they need to understand and what they need to do next. In credit reporting, the care behind that work can affect a person’s financial life long after a correction is made. That draws me to Experian’s Expert Compliance Risk Advisor role.

At Wells Fargo, I led analytics for Collateral Protection Insurance (CPI) and other Consumer Auto remediation programs representing $1B+ in exposure and customer impact. Across seven years of CPI work, I led development, recurring production, and regulatory response for proof-of-insurance intake, redress recalculation, credit-reporting corrections, and tax reporting, supporting consent-order clearance efforts. My validated SAS feeds and dashboard evidence connected complaint handlers and Audit investigators to account-level remediation decisions.

I also led 15+ additional remediation workstreams, including Tier 1 servicemember benefits and disaster relief. I aligned financial calculations with Finance policy and credit-reporting adjustments with FCRA guidance, brought recommendations to senior Decision Forums, and documented resolved decisions. Peer review, mentoring, training, and reusable guidance helped colleagues carry the work forward consistently.

At the Census Bureau, I coordinated a six-analyst nightly production workflow and built HTML/JavaScript and Excel/VBA tools that optimized Capital Expenditures and Retail Trade survey cycles. Analysts could focus on high-impact nonresponse and validation; managers could track progress and reallocate resources as priorities changed.

At the OCC, I supported 16 Credit Risk Economists and mentored junior colleagues. I built validated histories covering 875,516 loans for economist-led research on the risks of long-term auto loans, published in the Journal of Credit Risk, where the authors acknowledged my research support. My Tableau views supported model review and stress-testing analysis.

My personal portfolio makes that experience explicit. The Enterprise Credit Decisioning Strategy Simulator provides a framework for testing credit-risk strategies before production, using configurable synthetic data. I directed AI-assisted development with business requirements and validation evidence available for review. Power BI supports version control validation, with DAX narratives connecting application-level changes to the workstreams, such as mortgage affordability, that drove the result.

Merchant-Sales-Based-Financing models short-term merchant financing repaid through a fixed percentage of daily sales, using synthetic data. I defined funding and monitoring requirements, challenged results, and coordinated correction and separate review. Power BI connects merchant sales, liquidity, proposed terms, and DAX-generated decision explanations.

I think of this work as TRUTH made visible: tangible evidence of how I think, challenge assumptions, and decide whether a result is ready to use. My Human-in-the-Loop case study documents how I directed parallel AI-assisted workstreams and separate review while retaining responsibility for the questions, evidence, and final acceptance.

At Experian, I would bring disciplined evidence gathering, constructive challenge, and clear communication to regulatory requests and corrective actions. I would work with compliance, legal, and business partners to translate requirements into practical review steps, trace decisions to supporting evidence, and keep leaders informed about unresolved risks.

I would welcome the opportunity to contribute with that same care, analytical rigor, and accountability.

Warm regards,

Andrew R. Goad
