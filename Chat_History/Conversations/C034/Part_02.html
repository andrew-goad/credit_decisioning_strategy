6. How APR Should Actually Be Generated

APR should not just be:

random between min and max

Instead:

APR = function of:

  • product_type
  • credit_score band
  • small noise factor
  • rate environment shift

Conceptual structure

base_apr =
    product_base_rate
  + risk_premium(score_band)
  + noise
  + rate_environment_shift

Example (Auto Loan)

  • Base rate: 6%
  • Risk premium:
    • super-prime: +0–1%
    • prime: +1–3%
    • near-prime: +3–6%
    • subprime: +6–10%
  • Noise: ±0.5–1.5%

This produces:

  • realistic spread
  • smooth transitions
  • no hard jumps

7. Why This Matters for Payment Burden

APR directly feeds:

[ payment = f(amount, term, APR) ]

So:

  • higher APR → higher payment
  • higher payment → higher PTI
  • higher PTI → higher PD
